About Us
The office of the Accountant General Sindh is part of the Controller General of Accounts. It provides an independent, nonpartisan Financial Statement and Appropriation Accounts of the Government of Sindh. The Office of the Accountant General Sindh acts as a financial advisor to the Government of Sindh.
That’s valuable information about the role and responsibilities of the Office of the Accountant General Sindh within the Controller General of Accounts. Their provision of independent financial statements and advisory services contributes to greater transparency and accountability in the government’s financial management.
