Administration Wing

Administration Wing is responsible for ensuring that CDA has the right personnel, resources, and support systems in place to carry out its operations effectively. Additionally, it oversees the allocation of quarters and residences to employees. Furthermore, the secretariat takes charge of all municipal functions, ensuring compliance with Municipal By-laws.
Capital Hospital
The Capital Development Authority (CDA) in Islamabad, Pakistan, has embarked on an ambitious project to establish a state-of-the-art hospital to cater to the growing healthcare needs of the city. The hospital aims to provide world-class medical facilities and services to residents and improve the overall healthcare infrastructure in the region. It envisions a modern facility equipped with cutting-edge technology, well-equipped operation theaters, specialized departments, and advanced diagnostic facilities. The hospital intends to serve as a hub for medical excellence, offering a wide range of medical specialties and services including emergency care, surgeries, maternal and child health care, critical care units, and rehabilitation services. Furthermore, it is designed to house skilled medical professionals, highly-trained staff, and visiting consultants who can deliver the highest quality of care to patients. CDA recognizes the importance of public health and is ensuring that residents are provided with accessible, affordable, and reliable healthcare services within Islamabad.

2. Mandate
The primary mission of the Capital Hospital is to provide safe, high-quality, cost-effective, tertiary healthcare consistent with patient needs, in a setting with highly trained professionals and state of the art medical infrastructure. At hospital a full-range of services, from Emergency Ambulance Service, emergency room care to surgery to rehabilitation are provided. Capital Hospital healthcare system includes outpatient medical specialist services and clinics, inpatient services and accidents and emergency service, which work together to provide a broad range of inpatient and outpatient services. Capital Hospital recognizes its responsibility, and with guidance from top management of Capital Development Authority (CDA), and local experts in the medical field, to plan for the future healthcare needs in its service area through continuous strategic planning. Our system shall continue to provide high-quality healthcare and promote good health in the community. At Capital Hospital, it is believed that providing truly exceptional healthcare involves more than just treating a symptom, it means caring for a person - a person whose needs include a comfortable environment, a friendly face and a reassuring touch in addition to state-of-the-art medical technology and expertise. Our dedicated team of caregivers - doctors, nurses, technicians, administrators and more - are confident in our ability to provide excellent healthcare that incorporates human as well as state of the art technology.

Security Directorate
The directorate is responsible for a wide range of critical functions, including:
Investigation/inquires pertaining to Estate Wing. Engineering Wing, Environment Wing, Planning & Designed Wing, Finance Wing, Admin Wing and Law Wing as well as addressing complaints received directly or through the CDA Board Members.
Inquiries and Reporting: Conducting inquires as assigned by the CDA Board, gathering information on security and corruption issues, and submitting security and situational reports to the Chairman, CDA.
Reporting irregularities: reporting malpractices and irregularities, such as corruption, misuse of resources, manpower issues, attendance monitoring, pilferage and shortcoming. Implementing orders from the Board where necessary.
Chairman's Support: Providing assistance to the Chairman of CDA as required.
Headquarters Security: Ensuring the security, watch and ward of the CDA Headquarters.
Employee verification: Verifying the backgrounds and credentials of CDA employees, including certificates and degrees, as well as status of affected parties.
Night Patrolling: Conducting night patrols for added security.
Store Checks: Periodically checking the CDA's stores.
Civil Administration Liaison: Maintaining communication and coordination with civil administration.
Efficiently performing these duties, the security directorate plays a key role in maintaining the integrity, security and operations of the CDA/MCI and contributes to its overall effectiveness.
