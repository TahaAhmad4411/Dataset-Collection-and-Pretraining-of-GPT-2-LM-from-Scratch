Editorial & Advisory Board
The Editorial Board of Federal Law Journal plays a vital role in advancing quality knowledge within fields of legal research. They do this by maintaining and improving the quality of work the journal publishes, the journal’s reputation and the integrity of its peer review process.
To support this role, Federal Judicial Academy sets out the minimum standards for the Editorial Board which have responsibility for decisions on contents of the journal to ensure publication of quality and trustworthy content. The Editorial Board consists of:
