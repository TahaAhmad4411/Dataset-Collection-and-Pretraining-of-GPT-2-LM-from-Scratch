Commemorating Quaid E Azam's 146th Birth Anniversary: CBD Punjab's Glorious Tribute to the Father of the Nation
The Central Business District (CBD Punjab), has paid tribute to the founder of Pakistan, Quaid E Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah, on his 146th birth anniversary by installing a 50 ft. portrait of him at the CBD Punjab project site in Lahore.
Mr. Imran Amin, CEO PCBDDA, expressed that the authority is dedicated to fulfilling Quaid's vision of making Pakistan an economic and welfare state by taking it to new heights of development and prosperity. 
The portrait installation is a token of homage to Quaid-e-Azam's contributions to the nation, and PCBDDA will continue to strive toward achieving Quaid's aspirations. This portrait is also a testament to CBD Punjab's commitment to driving Pakistan's development to new heights, ultimately realizing the vision of a prosperous and economically thriving nation.
