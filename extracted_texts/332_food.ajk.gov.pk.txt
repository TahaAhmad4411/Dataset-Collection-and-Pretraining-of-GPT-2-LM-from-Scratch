about the food department
minister food:
Ch Muhammad Akbar
Welcome to the official website of the Food Department, where we are committed to ensuring food security and safeguarding the well-being of our citizens
I am deeply honored to serve as the Minister of the Food Department, and it is my privilege to address you through this platform. Our website is your gateway to information, services, and resources that aim to enhance your understanding of our department's mission and the services we provide.
Ensuring that every citizen has access to safe, nutritious, and affordable food is our primary concern. We are working diligently to implement programs and policies that bolster food security across our nation.
We aim to build a nation where no one goes to bed hungry and where food is a source of pride, health, and economic growth.Your feedback and suggestions are crucial in helping us improve and better serve our community.
secretary food:Sardar Sohail Azam
It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to the official website of the Food Department. Our website serves as a vital bridge between our department and the citizens we proudly serve.
The Food Department is committed to ensuring food security. Your safety is paramount to us. We are resolute in enforcing stringent food safety standards to guarantee that the food you consume is of the highest quality. We have designed this website to make it simple for you to access various services, including information on food programs, licensing and permits, and much more. We're committed to transparency and keeping you informed about our initiatives and programs.
We are continuously working to enhance our services and make information more accessible. We look forward to serving you and working together for the betterment of our food systems and the welfare of our citizens.
