Procedure for Application of Foreign Degree Equivalence
Go to eservices.hec.gov.pk
Signup to create your account.
Please make sure you have an active email ID and cellphone number.
After successful account signup, Login to your account
Go to Equivalence Services
Foreign Degree Recognition/Equivalence.
Fill the following:
Personal details
Details of degrees
Equivalence Details
Document upload
Verify Details
Submit the application.
After submission of application form, click on “Task Assigned" at bottom of the main page>Pay the fee using 1Link via any mobile banking app>verify payment.
Documents required:
For Bachelor's degree application
Degree and Transcript for which equivalence is required.
Intermediate/HSSC/FSc/grade 12 certificate or Equivalent qualification certificate issued by IBCC equivalence in case of foreign qualifications.
Matric/SSC/grade 10 or Equivalent qualification certificate issued by IBCC equivalence in case of foreign qualifications.
For Master's degree application
Degree and Transcript for which equivalence is required.
Bachelor's degree duly attested by HEC.
In case of foreign qualification, the applicant needs to get equivalence of his Bachelor's degree first.
Intermediate/HSSC/FSc/grade 12 certificate or Equivalent qualification certificate issued by IBCC equivalence in case of foreign qualifications.
Matric/SSC/grade 10 or Equivalent qualification certificate issued by IBCC equivalence in case of foreign qualifications.
For PhD degree application
Degree and Transcript for which equivalence is required.
Master's degree/previous Pakistani qualifications/degrees duly attested by HEC.
In case of foreign qualifications, the applicant needs to get equivalence first.
Intermediate/HSSC/FSc/grade 12 certificate or Equivalent qualification certificate issued by IBCC equivalence in case of foreign qualifications.
Matric/SSC/grade 10 or Equivalent qualification certificate issued by IBCC equivalence in case of foreign qualifications.​​
