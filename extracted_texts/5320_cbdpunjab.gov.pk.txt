CBD PUNJAB JOINS THE FIGHT AGAINST DENGUE BY RAISING AWARENESS
CBD Punjab is dedicated to the well-being of the people of Punjab. CBD Punjab observed July 15, 2023, as "Dengue Day" in alignment with the directives of the Government of Punjab.
CBD Punjab is dedicated to the well-being of the people of Punjab. CBD Punjab observed July 15, 2023, as "Dengue Day" in alignment with the directives of the Government of Punjab. This initiative aimed to raise awareness about dengue fever, including its symptoms, available treatments, and the necessary safety precautions to prevent its spread.
Dengue fever is a significant public health concern, and CBD Punjab is committed to actively contributing to its mitigation by disseminating awareness through its communication platforms. By observing Dengue Day, CBD Punjab strives to educate and empower the public with knowledge to protect themselves and their communities.
In support of this cause, CBD Punjab organized a Dengue Awareness Walk that brought together directorate heads and staff members of CBD Punjab. This walk served as a tangible demonstration of CBD Punjab's dedication to community engagement in the battle against this disease.
Brigadier (R) Mansoor Janjua, COO of CBD Punjab, shared his thoughts on the occasion, stating, "The health and well-being of the people of Punjab are of paramount importance to CBD Punjab. By observing Dengue Day and participating in the Dengue Awareness Walk, we aim to empower individuals with the knowledge and resources needed to protect themselves and their loved ones from dengue fever. Together, we can create a safer and healthier Punjab."
CBD Punjab remains steadfast in its mission of fostering sustainable development and prioritizing the welfare of the people it serves. Initiatives like Dengue Day exemplify CBD Punjab's commitment to creating a safer and healthier environment for all residents of Punjab.
