Message from the Chairman
×
Mr. Tauqeer Aslam
It is privileged for me to be a chairperson of Appellate Tribunal Inland Revenue of Pakistan. Appellate Tribunal Inland Revenue is a quasi judicial institution specializes in dealing with Appeals under the Direct and Indirect Taxes and dispensing impartial, easy and speedy justice. The orders passed by the ATIR are final, a reference lies to the High Court only if a substantial question of law arises for determination.
The first and perhaps most obvious advantage of a website is the potential for reaching a wider public at large. I feel proud by launching Appellate Tribunal Inland Revenue website and confident that taxpayers, Revenue Department and other community can easily access information about ATIR, its benches as well as judicial and accountant members and much more. They can spectator what functions, duties performed by ATIR under fiscal statute.
I am indebted and really appreciated to the committee who developed this website for taxpayers and other users and confident that the exhaustive information containing in it shall useful for public at large. I am looking forward to seeing more innovative works in this respect.
(Tauqeer Aslam)
