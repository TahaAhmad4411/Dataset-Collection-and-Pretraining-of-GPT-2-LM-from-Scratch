PRIME MINISTER'S
EDUCATION REFORM PROGRAM
The Prime Minister's Education Reform Program is dedicated to upgrading school facilities, refurbishing the buildings, enhancing teaching standards, and revitalizing education in cities as well as the underdeveloped areas of Pakistan. This initiative empowers communities, fosters innovation, and builds a brighter future for the nation's youth.
PM'S FOCUS ON EDUCATION
Prime Minister Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif has announced an "Education Emergency" across Pakistan to enroll approximately 26 million children who are currently out of school, aiming to transform Pakistan into one of the most educated countries in the world. The Prime Minister emphasized his commitment to addressing this critical challenge during a National Conference on Education Emergency held in Islamabad.
EDUCATION REFORM PROJECTS
The Education Reform Projects are the cornerstone of our mission to transform Pakistan's educational landscape. By modernizing infrastructure, enhancing teaching standards, and expanding opportunities, these projects are paving the way for a future where every child has access to quality education and a brighter tomorrow.
The Pakistan Dream: Unstoppable
This transformative initiative rebuilds educational infrastructure, fosters innovation, and empowers communities across Pakistan. From the rapid reconstruction of schools in Waziristan and Chilas to the establishment of technology parks, smart schools, and AI-driven systems, the program is reshaping education. With a focus on financial literacy, STEAM, entrepreneurship, and holistic development, it paves the way for a brighter, more progressive future. Upcoming projects include solarizing schools, enhancing digital learning, and introducing advanced technology education across the nation.

Sectoral Presentation
MINISTRY OF FEDERAL EDUCATION & PROFESSIONAL TRAINING
This presentation outlines key reforms focused on 21st-century skills, including high-impact courses at top universities and coding/AI integration in schools. Curriculum updates introduce financial literacy and entrepreneurship, while AI-driven assessments replace traditional exams. Infrastructure upgrades, fast-track teacher recruitment, digitalized training, and improved student health and nutrition underscore the comprehensive approach to enhancing education nationwide.
Renovation Work of 167 Educational Institutions and 5 Area Education offices
This government-funded project, set to complete by June 30, 2025, aims to renovate 167 educational institutions across rural and urban areas of ICT. The project focuses on upgrading 107 rural and 60 urban schools, benefiting both primary schools and colleges. Key improvements include constructing 519 new classrooms, enhancing facilities with vibrant designs, IT labs, libraries, and upgraded infrastructure. Special attention is given to girls’ and boys’ institutes, ensuring modern, safe, and well-equipped learning environments.
HIGH-TECH COURSES IN 16 DEGREE COLLEGES AND 5 IT LABS
The Ministry of Federal Education & Professional Training has undertaken a comprehensive initiative to elevate the technological capabilities of 16 degree colleges by renovating and retrofitting them with the latest IT infrastructure. In addition, 5 brand-new IT labs have been constructed to support this modernization effort. These state-of-the-art facilities now host advanced courses in High-Tech fields such as Blockchain, Cloud Computing, and Artificial Intelligence. This initiative is designed to impart cutting-edge skills to Pakistan's youth, preparing them for success in a technology-driven future and contributing to the nation's development in the global digital economy.
EDUCATION REFORMS
INITIATIVES GOVERNMENT
OF PUNJAB
The Government of Punjab’s education reforms focus on several key areas: The School Meal Program provides nutritious meals to combat child stunting, boosting enrollment and health. Smart Schools and enhanced STEAM, Entrepreneurship, and Computer Education are modernizing learning. Pink Buses offer safe transport for female students and working women. The Data Science, AI, and Blockchain Certification Course prepares students for advanced tech careers. The Summer Fiesta! program includes career counseling, game development, IT boot camps, fitness activities, and mental health support, fostering comprehensive student development.

SPECIAL INITIATIVES by mOFEPT
The Ministry has launched initiatives including the School Meal Program, solarization, and rebuilding of flood-affected schools. Key projects involve Smart Schools with advanced tech courses, Financial Literacy, and schools' beautification. Efforts also include Makerspaces, blended learning hubs, and skill development for 650,000 youth. Distance learning is enhanced through the Teleschool Pakistan App and E-Taleem portal. Additional programs support mobile schools, teacher training, and inclusive curriculum development.
GIRLS' EDUCATION
IN PAKISTAN
STATISTICS & TRENDS
FOR 2022-23
This research explores key aspects of girls' education in Pakistan for 2022-23, highlighting the ongoing need for gender parity in access. It examines the gradual rise in enrollments, institutional growth, and the increasing participation of girls in STEM fields. Additionally, the study addresses the representation of female educators and regional disparities affecting educational outcomes.
PRIME MINISTER'S SPECIAL INITIATIVES
This comprehensive program focuses on transforming education through key initiatives such as the School Meal Program, upgrading 16 colleges into digital training institutes, and establishing STEAM, AI, and robotics labs. Additional efforts include the renovation of 167 schools, the launch of the Free Pink Bus initiative, and the creation of 5 Software Technology Parks. Other highlights include the introduction of coding and AI fellows in primary schools, foreign language courses, evening school programs, and the establishment of art, podcast, and mind sports rooms.
