Dr. Mujeeb-ur-Rahman Khan, Hon'ble Member
Dr. Mujeeb-ur-Rahman Khan, Hon'ble Member
Dr. Mujeeb Ur Rahman Khan joined the Federal Service Tribunal as Executive Member in November 2021. The Honorable Member is a retired Inspector General of Police (IGP) of BS-22. He belongs to Balochistan and is a PSP Officer of 16
th
CTP Batch of Civil Service. He is a Dentist by his educational qualification. He served as Additional Inspector General of Police in Balochistan and Inspector General of Police in Railways and Gilgit Baltistan. He remained Additional Director General and Director General of FIA also.
