INSTRUCTIONS FOR AUTHORS
Before submitting a manuscript to Geologica, an international journal of Earth Sciences, authors are required to prepare their manuscript following the instructions given below.
Submission requirement
Check list before submission of the manuscript
a.     Items that should be present at the time of submission
One author to be designated as the corresponding author (also provide contact details including email and postal address
Manuscript (text file, including keywords, figures with captions, and tables with description and footnotes if needed)
Ensure all figure and tables are properly cited in the text and match the files provided
Provide supplemental files (where applicable)
Recommended Formatting (Font: Times New Roman, Font size: 12px, Text alignment: Justified)
Further considerations
Manuscript has been “spell and grammar checked”
All references mentioned in the Reference List are cited in the text, and vice versa
Permission has been obtained for use of copyrighted material from other sources (including the Internet)
Referee suggestions and contact details provided
b.     Manuscript preparation
Text file of the manuscript shall be prepared in Microsoft Word document (composed on a A4 210 x 297 mm page-setup, with 25 mm margins on top/bottom and left/right, doubly spaced lines, 12 Font size, Times New Romans, with continuous line-numbers shown throughout, and paginated at bottom center) and submitted at the “GEOLOGICA” manuscript center (Editorial Manager of Geologica):
https://geologica.gov.pk/
c.     Article Structure
Title
Concise and conveying a strong message regarding the contents
(Capitalize, font size-14, bold and centered)
Example:
The Location Of The Main Central Thrust In The Northwest Himalayas Of Pakistan: Tectonic Implications
Author Names
Full name(s) of all authors and their affiliation should be clearly mentioned with at least one author as the corresponding author (provide email of corresponding author).
Example:
Corresponding author and email:
Naghma Haider,
naghma.haider@gmail.com
Abstract
A short and brief summary (not more than 300 words) that explains the study topic, its contents, methodology, and main results/conclusions, that should be elaborated in details in the main text (Times New Romans, font size 10, typed in
italics, centered
).
Example:
A number of workers have proposed different positions for the Main Central Thrust in the northwest Himalaya of Pakistan A number of workers have proposed different positions for the Main Central Thrust in the northwest Himalaya of Pakistan A number of workers have proposed different positions for the Main Central Thrust in the northwest Himalaya of Pakistan A number of workers have proposed different positions for the Main Central Thrust in the northwest Himalaya of Pakistan A number of workers have proposed different positions for the Main Central Thrust in the northwest Himalaya of Pakistan A number of workers have proposed different positions for the Main Central Thrust in the northwest Himalaya of Pakistan A number of workers have proposed different positions for the Main Central Thrust in the northwest Himalaya of Pakistan.
Keywords
Provide 3 to 6 keywords (font size 10)
Example:
Tectonics, Main Central Thrust, NW Himalayas, Pakistan
Introduction
Clearly present objectives of the work, provide a brief background of the study (avoid detailed literature survey), and what has been achieved in the study (Times New Roman, font size 12, bold, and left aligned).
Geological Background
Details on study area or related matter
Methods/Analytical procedure
Procedures, analytical methods, materials, instruments etc. should be clearly stated.
Results
Provide detailed results, data, figures, tables amd so on.
Discussion
Discuss the rationale of study, results/data, figures, observations, and supported with past literature or relevant areas.
Conclusion(s)
Brief and concise summary of the study.
Acknowledgements
Provide funding source, and other relevant info if applicable

References to be quoted in text
References should be cited in the text as (single author: Author, 2020), if two authors (Author1 & Author2, 2020), and multiple as (Auhtor1 et al., 2020). If cited altogether use semi-colon between/among various authors works following the chronological order (Author1, 2010;Author1 & Author2, 2018; Auhtor1 et al., 2020).
Tables
Tables should be prepared in Microsoft Excel, Word, or data in CSV format, with no internal formatting, line artworks or internal line-breaks. Provide all the data in single sheet and to be printed on a single page if possible.
Illustrations
Provide all illustrations/figures in electronic format at the time of manuscript submission, with resolutions no smaller than 300 dpi. If the quality is low, editorial office may contact for better resolution files. Acceptable formats are eps, jpg, tiff, or pdf. Illustrations of each file must be provided separately.
