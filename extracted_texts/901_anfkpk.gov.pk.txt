Anti Narcotics Force Peshawar at Torkham boarder conducted different operations
Anti Narcotics Force Peshawar at Torkham boarder conducted different operations and apprehended 1x afghan national namely Barkat Ullah and 2x residents of Peshawar namely Rizwan Ullah and Saima.
During these operations 2 KGs of Opium, 9.600 KGs of Heroine, 2.600 KGs of Hashish and 4.033 KGs of Ecstasy & Xanax tablets were recovered and seized.
Cases has been registered and further investigation in progress.
