Accounts
The Accountant General’s Office performs functions related to government expenditures, receipts, accounting and overall public financial management. It also deals with Compilation of monthly accounts and preparation of statutory Financial Statements (
Appropriation and Financial Statements
) of government of AJ&K in accordance with legal provisions and generally accepted accounting standards prescribed by Auditor General and Controller General of Accounts.
Office of the Accountant General
The Accountant General AJ&K is responsible for the centralized accounting and reporting of financial transactions of AJ&K.
