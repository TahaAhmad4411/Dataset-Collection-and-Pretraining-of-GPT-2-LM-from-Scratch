Introduction
The Livestock & Dairy Development Department (Lⅅ) Punjab is a department of  Government of the Punjab - Pakistan.
                                The Directorate of Animal Disease Diagnostic. Reporting and Surveillance. L&OD  Punjab has network of one Provincial
                                Laboratory at Lahore, 8 divisional Laboratories, 27 districts and 5 Poultry Laboratories. All these laboratories along with
                                36 districts heads of the livestock and their Deputy District Livestock offices at tehsil levels and the livestock farmers
                                are being connected with ADRS-info system through mobile and web based applications  for collecting,
                                processing, diagnosing and reporting the animal disease data in the province.
M/S Awami Systems Technologies [AST] as the technology service provider is responsible for developing and maintaining the
                                ADRSinfo System of the Directorate of Animal Disease Diagnostic Reporting and Surveillance, Lⅅ, Punjab.
We Are Committed to Protect the Privacy
We collect necessary information about the Livestock farmers and their livestock for providing the best possible services.
                                This policy indicates the types of processes that may result in data being collected about the farmers. The use of this system
                                through various applications by the farmers, and veterinary officers and para-vet staff gives us the right to collect that
                                information for furthering the objective of livestock & Dairy Development Department Punjab, and it will not be misused.
Information Collected
We may collect all or any Information like Name, CNIC Number, Mobile Number, Location, Address, SMS, Livestock Population,
                                Disease Type, Sign/ Symptoms of the sick animals. In order to provide quick services we also seek access to Camera, Gallery,
                                Location Information and other information that may be needed.
Information Use
The Information collected is primarily used to serve the livestock of the farmers in relation
                                to treatment, guidance and taking necessary measures for preventing the disease outbreak in the area. All reasonable
                                precautions are taken to prevent unauthorized
                                access to this information
Right of access
If any person has concern or have any questions about Personal Data, the right to
                                request access to the Personal Data which we hold or process. We will then provide Information about the data
                                that are being processed and on the source of those data.
