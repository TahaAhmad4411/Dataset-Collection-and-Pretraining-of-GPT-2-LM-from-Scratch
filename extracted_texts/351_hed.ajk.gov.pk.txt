Our Mission
The realization of the higher education department's vision of "enlightened and prospering Azad Jammu & Kashmir by reinforcing knowledge economy" rests on the shift from access to quality which is evident from the key initiatives it has taken in recent past.
Our Values
The values of Higher Education is to promote development of an enlightened and prospering Azad Jammu & Kashmir by reinforcing knowledge economy along with a focus on equitable and quality learning.
