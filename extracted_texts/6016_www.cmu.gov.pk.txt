The CMU Team
Common Management Unit aims at accelerating the National and Provincial response to control AIDS, TB and Malaria and subsequently eliminate the three deadly diseases from the country as per the governmental commitment to achieve Sustainable Development Goals by 2030.
Pakistan is a high burden country in terms of patient load in AIDS, TB and Malaria. CMU Team is working hard to solve issues in these three deadly diseases. We are in line with the targets of SDG 3 to which Pakistan is a signatory.
Meet Our Team
Under leadership of Ministry of National Health Services Regulations and Coordination, Government of Pakistan and support from our partners we aim to eradicate AIDS, TB and Malaria from the country.
