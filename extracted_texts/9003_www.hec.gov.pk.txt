JSTOR for research: 2024 update
Learn about the latest time-saving features and functionality on JSTOR. These make finding, keeping track of, and sharing all kinds of content on JSTOR easy and efficient. 
Explore:
 - New and more efficient ways to discover books, journals, and research reports
 - How to unearth millions of primary sources from contributing collections including audio and video files
 - Finding and saving Images from museums and collections worldwide 
 - Updates to the JSTOR Workspace, the place to organize and share content on JSTOR
This 55 minute webinar is appropriate for librarians, researchers and teaching faculty.

Webinar on Books at JSTOR Overview
You know ease of use is critical to the discovery and use of scholarly monographs. That's why Books at JSTOR offers an unlimited, DRM-free access model that enables you to cross-search ebooks, journal articles, and primary sources—all in one place. Savings on all ebooks and flexible acquisition options are also offered to fit collection strategies and budgets.
Register now for our 30-minute webinar,
Books at JSTOR overview: Ease of use, affordability, and flexible acquisition models
. Dates and times are:
Wednesday, 18 September at 05:30 PM to 06:30 PM PST
