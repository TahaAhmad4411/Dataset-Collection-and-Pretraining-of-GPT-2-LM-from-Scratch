Services
Same Day Delivery Service (SDDS)
Pakistan Post has started new service for big metropolitan cities for delivery of Express Post articles on the day of booking.
Service Features
Important and urgent items that need immediate delivery.
Booking upto 20 kg and delivery upto 5 kg at doorstep.
Delivery beyond 5 kg and upto 20 kg on counter.
Preferred choice for Business to Business needs.
Dedicated handling & support desk.
Service will be available in selected areas of following cities.
The delivery information will be communicated through SMS.
