Besides mandatory as well as vocational trainings on legal and judicial education, Federal Judicial Academy provides a platform for the judicial officers, researchers, academicians, practitioners, court staff and students of law to impart and share knowledge in the form of high quality empirical and theoretical research papers, case studies, literature reviews and book reviews. Federal Law Journal is a high quality open access peer reviewed research journal that is published by Federal Judicial Academy with the aim to disseminate advanced legal knowledge to a wider audience to generate new insights and shed new light on existing laws, different legal issues and legal framework of Pakistan.
