Anti Narcotics Force (ANF) Peshawar, intercepted a vehicle and recovered 6 Kg’s of Hashish and 1.2 Kg of Opium
Anti Narcotics Force (ANF) Peshawar, on a tip off, intercepted a Vitz car bearing Reg#BA-1552 near Tatara Park Peshawar and arrested 2x Accused namely
Irfan Ullah s/o Ehsan Ullah and Muhammad Hizar Hayat khan S/O Sher Zamin. Upon search 6 KGs of Hashish along with 1.2 KGs of Opium concealed in plastic bags were recovered from the front doors of the said vehicle.
The said drugs were being smuggled from District Khyber to Peshawar.
Case has been registered, and further investigation in progress.
