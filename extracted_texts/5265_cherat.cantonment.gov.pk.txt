Building Committee is a Sub-Committee of the Cantonment Board constituted U/S 44 of the Cantonments Act, 1924 to deal with the following applications belongs to properties of private land and outside Bazar area: -
Application received U/S 179 of the Cantonments Act, 1924 for seeking approval of the Board for erection / re-erection of residential & commercial buildings.
Applications U/S 183-A of the Cantonments Act, 1924 for grant of extension in time limit for completion of residential and commercial buildings.
