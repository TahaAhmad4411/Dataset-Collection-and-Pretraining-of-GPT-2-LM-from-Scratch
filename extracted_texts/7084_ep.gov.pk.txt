Be Alert! 🔥 Pakistan Post has its website for online tracking purpose https://ep.gov.pk/. Any other link or website for tracking purpose does not belong to Pakistan Post. Pakistan post collects duty and taxes imposed on articles through its own manual system. There is no online system / call centre to collect any amount from customers.
