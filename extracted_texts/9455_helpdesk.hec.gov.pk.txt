How to Update your academic Profile accurately
Posted by Higher Education Comission 2 on 17 November 2015 11:25 AM
Only those students who registered themselves in the previous Prime Minister's National laptop scheme but didn't receive their laptop will be eligible to Update Profile.
For proper Understanding
Download Manual
OR
For video guide click on
New Registration
OR
Follow the instructions to update your profile:
Step 1:
Open the link
http://pmnls.hec.gov.pk
and Select your city and university and enter your CNIC to access your information.
Step 2:
Click on
Update
.
Note:
Any change of email request will
NOT
be entertained.
In case you forgot your personal email password , see general instructions.
In case you forgot the password of helpdesk portal, you can retrieve it by using FORGET Option.
Step3:
Click on
Update Academic Profile
and fill the form completely.
Note:
You will be allowed to update your academic details only
ONCE
so, please enter your correct information. Once you click on
Submit
, you will
NOT
be able to access this option again.
if you cannot find Your relevant department/Degree Program then contact focal person of the university to add and update.
Update All Academic Data of your Profile as on 30th June 2016, as per the policy of Prime Minister's National Laptop Scheme (Passed exam i.e Annual/Semester).
Then, Click
Submit
to update your profile.
General Instructions:
In case you forgot the password of your personal email account, following links will be helpful to reset it:
Gmail:
https://www.google.com/accounts/recovery/verifyuser
Yahoo:
https://edit.yahoo.com/mforgot?intl=us&lang=en-US
Hotmail:
https://account.live.com/ResetPassword
Others : You can ask that service provider about that .
You can check your status from this link:
http://pmnls.hec.gov.pk
or contact your university focal person.
Save your time by keeping a track of your password in future, in order to avoid any inconvenience.
