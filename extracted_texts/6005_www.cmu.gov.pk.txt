on High-Level Meeting on Health: Coordinator to PM and Federal Secretary MOH Discuss National Progress with CMU and Global Fund CT on Combating TB, Malaria, and HIV/AIDS
Dr. Malik Mukhtar Ahmad Bharath, Coordinator to the Prime Minister on Health, and Mr. Nadeem Mahbub, Federal Secretary of the Ministry of National Health Services, Regulations & Coordination, held a meeting today with Ms. Izaskun Gaviria, Senior Fund Portfolio Manager of the Global Fund. The meeting, held in Islamabad on 18th September 2024, focused on reviewing efforts to combat TB, Malaria, HIV/AIDS, with a particular emphasis on programmatic progress.
