Eligibility criteria for 4 Years-BS Program:
The applicants must possess a domicile/local certificate of residence of District Gwadar.
Must have completed 12 years of formal education (FA/FSc/ICS/I.COM/DAE) or equivalent.
The pre-requisite qualification must be acquired before the closing date. Students already enrolled in BS Program prior to the advertisement (maximum one academic year/ session) may apply.
The applicants who are enrolled in any Public Sector University of Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Sindh are eligible to apply for the scholarship.
The candidates already availing of any other scholarships are not eligible to apply.
Maximum age is 22 years as on closing date.​
