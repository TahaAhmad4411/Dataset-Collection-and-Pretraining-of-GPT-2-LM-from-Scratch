BALANCE BETWEEN ASPIRATIONS AND CAPABILITIES 2 MAY 23
Professor Doctor Javed Iqbal delivered an online lecture to the student officers on Balance between Aspirations and Capabilities. He opened his talk by explaining different kinds of aspirations and their likely outcomes. He highlighted the difference between needs, aspirations and their connection to human body and spirit. He implored upon the audience to explore their individual purpose of life. He highlighted that one must strive to realize his aspirations, however, should remain contented with the outcome. He also emphasized the need of carrying out self-assessment in order to align aspirations with one’s capabilities. Towards the end, he stressed upon the value of self-reflection and corrective feedback. The talk was followed by an interactive session, in which, Student Officers asked several insightful questions, which were candidly responded to by the Guest Speaker.
