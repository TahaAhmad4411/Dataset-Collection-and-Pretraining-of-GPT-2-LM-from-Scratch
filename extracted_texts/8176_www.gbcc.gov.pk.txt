Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Court Promotes Mr. Zia-ur-Rehman to District & Sessions Judge
25 Jul 2023
In a significant development, the Departmental Promotion Committee, duly approved by the Hon'ble Chief Justice of the Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Court, has recommended and approved the promotion of Mr. Zia-ur-Rehman, serving as Addl. District & Sessions Judge in Diamer (BPS-20), to the prestigious position of District & Sessions Judge (BPS-21) on an acting charge basis.
