Journey Through Ages
“A Journey Through Ages”, depicts an epoch of over a century at Command and Staff College, Quetta. This century, duly termed as a “Century of Excellence”, embodies a unique treasure of memories, experiences, landmark events and happenings of historic significance. The objective of this publication is to share rich legacy of the college with the readers through the effective way of pictorial communication.
