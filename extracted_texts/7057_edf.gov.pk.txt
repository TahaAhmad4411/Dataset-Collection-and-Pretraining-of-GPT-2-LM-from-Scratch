Who We Are
Export Development Fund (EDF) is an autonomous body under Ministry of Commerce, Government of Pakistan. It works under the administration of a Board of Administrators. The aim of EDF is to address bottlenecks faced by the exporters and producers of export goods & services in Pakistan with a view to increase the performance of export sector.
