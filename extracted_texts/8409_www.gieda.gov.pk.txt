Mass Human Resource Services and GIEDA Collaborate to Advance Gwadar's Development
Mass Human Resource Services and Gwadar Industrial Estate Development Authority (GIEDA) Host Business Event on the Future of CPEC in Gwadar Special Event Held at China Business Center in Gwadar.
An event titled “Explore and Uplift Gwadar” was organized to review the development of Balochistan, especially Gwadar and CPEC projects, and to discuss solutions for the challenges faced in these areas.
Various political, social, Govt officials and business personalities attended the event and expressed a keen interest in the development of Gwadar.

GWADAR INDUSTRIAL ESTATE DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY.
Gwadar Industrial Estate Development Authority (GIEDA) is a government organization established in 2004 with the aim of developing and managing industrial estates in the port city of Gwadar, located in the southwestern province of Balochistan, Pakistan.
WHY GIEDA
The Government of Balochistan is building up a modern bequest in Gwadar to satisfy the need for industrial plots in the port city, which is relied upon to have the generous potential for modern interest taking into account the chances offered by the foundation of profound seaport and development of significant thruways connecting Gwadar with Karachi, Quetta, Iran, and Central Asian nations. There are additional plans to stretch out the rail route to Gwadar
