February 15, 2024
An interactive session organized by AJK IT Board of all designated focal persons from various line departments of GOAJK held at PID Complex.
An interactive session organized by AJK IT Board of all designated focal persons from various line departments of GOAJK held today at Tourism Complex Muzaffarabad. The main purpose was to provide orientation and training regarding e-filing software and Biometric Time and Attendance Management. The session was Chaired by Director General IT Board Dr. Khalid Rafique accompanied by Mr.
Asim Khan
Director IT Board. Distribution of certificates amongst the participants were also presented upon completion of training as an acknowledgement and appreciation from DG IT Board on behalf of Secretary Information Technology GoAJ&K.
