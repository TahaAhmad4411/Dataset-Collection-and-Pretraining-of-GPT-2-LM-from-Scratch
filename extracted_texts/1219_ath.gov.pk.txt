PHYSIOTHERAPY DEPARTMENT
About Physiotherapy Department
Physiotherapy is a health care profession with an emphasis on the use of physical approaches for the promotion of health and the prevention, treatment and management of disease and disability.
Department of Physiotherapy of Ayub Teaching Hospital is one of the best physiotherapy departments of our Province. During year 2015 we managed 13,539 Patients.We provide a wide range of services which allows us to effectively treat a large variety of conditions.Together with a range of technical and support staff we deliver direct patient care, rehabilitation, treatment, diagnostics and health improvement interventions to restore and maintain optimal physical, sensory, psychological, cognitive and social functions.
