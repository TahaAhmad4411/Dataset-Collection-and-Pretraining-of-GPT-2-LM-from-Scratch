Services
The Home and Tribal Affairs Department offers the following services to the public:
Tribal Mediation
Facilitating negotiations between tribal communities to resolve conflicts.
Crime Reporting and Assistance
Enabling citizens to report crimes and receive assistance in cases of emergencies.
Public Safety Campaigns
Conducting workshops and awareness campaigns on public safety, crime prevention, and disaster preparedness.
Disaster Relief
Providing disaster management training and relief services during floods, earthquakes, and other emergencies.
