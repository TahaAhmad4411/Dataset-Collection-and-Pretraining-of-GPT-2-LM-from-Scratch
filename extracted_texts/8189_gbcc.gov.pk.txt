Record & Copy Branch
Gilgit Baltistan Chief Court
The Record Branch of the  Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Court is instrumental in managing and preserving the vast array of
Legal Documents
Cases Records
essential to the functioning of the judiciary. Tasked with the organization, maintenance, and retrieval of court records, this branch plays a critical role in ensuring the accuracy and accessibility of legal information. Records clerks meticulously catalog and index documents, ranging from case filings and judgments to court orders and pleadings, facilitating efficient retrieval for judges, lawyers, and litigants. Additionally, the Record Branch oversees the archiving and preservation of historical records, safeguarding the legal heritage of the region. Through their meticulous attention to detail and commitment to maintaining comprehensive and organized records, the Record Branch contributes significantly to the integrity and effectiveness of the Chief Court , enabling the seamless operation of the judiciary and the administration of justice.
