Hon’ble Mr. Justice Dr.Syed Muhammad Anwer, Acting Chief Justice, Federal Shariat Court of Pakistan addressed lawyers of the Islamabad Bar Association at District Courts on 10.09.2022, Islamabad. The Honourable Acting Chief Justice highlighted the jurisdiction of FSC and its role and importance in islamization of laws. He stated that The FSC is a unique institution with no parallel in the entire Muslim world, therefore, lawyers, especially those who have studied Sharia and Law, should come forward and play their part for Islamization of laws and upholding the rule of law with the help of FSC. He further stated that Islam believes in protecting the rights of minorities therefore, lawyers must strive to ensure that minorities are given their due right as Islam is the religion of peace and tranquility.The Honourable Acting Chief Justice also shed light on some of the landmark judgments of FSC, including the Judgment on Riba, surrogacy etc.The program was attended by a large numbers of lawyers.
Hon’ble Mr. Justice Dr.Syed Muhammad Anwer, Acting Chief Justice, Federal Shariat Court of Pakistan addressed lawyers and members of the civil society on Alternative Dispute Resolution at Islamabad High Court Bar Association on Friday dated 02.09.2022. The Honourable Acting Chief Justice stressed the need for opting Alternative Dispute Resolution and said that Islam always encourages sulha and compromise, giving specific references to Quran, Hadith and the practice of muslim jurists regarding Alternative Dispute Resolution. He also urged lawyers to avail Alternative Dispute Resolution before initiating litigation. The session was attended by lawyers, members of the civil society and students.

SHARIAH ACADEMY HOLDS CONFERENCE ON UNIVERSALITY OF ISLAMIC LAW
Two day conference on Universality of Islamic Law (International Criminal law and Human Rights) organized by the International Islamic University’s (IIU) Shariah Academy concluded here at the Faisal Masjid campus.The concluding ceremony was addressed by Judge, Federal Shariat Court, Justice Dr. Syed Muhammad Anwer. In his presidential address he said Islamic law is comprehensive and impartial. He opined that it is not prudent to have a comparison of the Islamic and western laws because Islam is a prolife religion where sanctity of life is top priority. He added that all the punishments in Islamic law are for the sake of deterrence. Islam is the ultimate way of guidance, he said. He appreciated the academy for arranging the activity and hoped that the academy shall continue such events in future.
