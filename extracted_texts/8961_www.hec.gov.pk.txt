Talent Hunt Program

Free Coaching Classes for MDCAT -2024
​Talent Hunt Program (THP) is initiated by the USAID-funded Merit and Needs Based Scholarship Program to encourage and prepare talented  students from low-income students who aspire for higher education but cannot apply for admission to top Pakistani universities due to the lack of finances required to enroll in for the professional entry test preparation workshops.  This initiative focuses on identifying bright students with an exceptional Matriculation and FA/FSc results ahead of time, investing in them by offering entry test preparation workshops for their admission to medical, engineering, and general universities of Pakistan.
Talent Hunt Program is not a scholarship for an undergraduate degree program.  (

Eligibility Criteria
​Student must be a Pakistan/AJ&K national
Must belongs to a low-income family
Must have secured ​​a minimum 70% in FSc. Pre-Medical (Part-I/II)
​Special preference will be given to female
​Students from rural/less developed districts in Pakistan
Students with disabilities
Special Instructions:
Talent Hunt Program is not a scholarship for studies in the medical college /university.
HEC will not accept hard copy of the application
HEC reserves the right to reject incomplete online applications
HEC may transfer any eligible applicant to nearby coaching center
Participation in Talent Hunt Program does not guarantee admission or academic scholarship in any Pakistani Medical College/University
Pr​ogram Coverage for students:
- Rs. 1500 per day for meals and transportation
- MDCAT modeled test will be conducted after the preparatory workshop
