March 7, 2023 at Jafza Jebel Ali Office Free Zone The Pakistan Business Council set up a meeting between Dr. Saifuddin Junejo, Chairman EPZA
admin
2023-03-10T10:02:48+00:00
March 7, 2023 at Jafza Jebel Ali Office Freezone
The Pakistan Business Council set up a meeting between Dr. Saifuddin Junejo, Chairman EPZA, and the Jafza Authority, represented by Mr. Abdul Rahman Ali and Mr. Hamad Al Saygh.
The meeting’s goals include boosting economic cooperation between Pakistan and the UAE and to learn more about the Freezone and the services it offers businesses.
Attended by:
Mr. Muhammad Idress
Mr. Waseem Akthar
Mr. Adeem Khan- Trade Investment Councilor – Pakistan Consulate
Mr. Muhamad Iqbal Dawood- President – Pakistan BC
Mr. Mustafa Hemani- Director PBC
Mr. Muhammad Nafees- VP PBC
Mr. Sultan Mahamood- Director
Mr. Saleem Zakaria- Director
Ms Ellanie Villena- Office Manager
