Vision/Mission
Vision
To ensure a safe, harmonious, and law-abiding society in Balochistan by promoting peace, managing tribal affairs, maintaining public order, and enhancing disaster preparedness to support sustainable socio-economic development.
Mission
Our mission is to safeguard public safety, promote peaceful coexistence among diverse communities, manage tribal affairs with sensitivity, and ensure the timely and effective management of disaster risks. We strive to collaborate with law enforcement, tribal leaders, and the public to create a secure environment for all citizens of Balochistan.
