ABOUT GIEDA
GIEDA (Gwadar Industrial Estate Development Authority) is an industrial project from Government of Balochistan. It is a commercial industrial project from govt of Balochistan. GIEDA is based on small, medium and large enterprises including industrial setups which will fulfil the need for all type of business activities and industries in Gwadar. Therefore, Gwadar industrial estate is an important part of CPEC projects designed to fulfil the need for industries in Balochistan.
Currently, Rates of commercial plots are available in acres and yards for sale, purchase, assessment and transfer in all the phases. Gwadar property has an amazing response from the investors from all over Pakistan. That is the reason, that Gwadar industrial estate development authority is a suitable choice for Pakistani businessmen to make an investment for future concerns.
