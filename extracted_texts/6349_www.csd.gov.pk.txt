CSD Loyalty Card
1
Eligibility
Every customer is eligible for issuance of CSD loyalty card.
2
Procedure
Procedure of Issuance of Card
Customer will buy the application form from any CSD shop worth Rs. 200.
After filling the necessary information, Application Form will be submitted to Manager CSD shop.
Manager will forward the Application Form to Head Office (IT Div, Card Section) for preparation of card
After necessary security of documents, IT Div will print the Card and dispatch to customer on his / her mailing address mentioned on form.
3
Activation
Card must be activated before 1st use.
Customer will provide CNIC to CSD rep for activation.
4
Discount
3% discount on shopping of grocery / retail (from CSD Retail Shops)
