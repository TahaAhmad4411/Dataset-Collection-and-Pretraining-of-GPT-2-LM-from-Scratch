Overview
Overview
Agriculture Research in Balochistan was initiated during 1891 with Planned Protection and Horticulture. In early stages, most of the work was on locust control, and on growing fruits and vegetables. The other job on Plant Protection was Quarantine and fumigation of Plants, Fruits, and Vegetables on the border of the Indian Subcontinent when these materials were coming from Afghanistan.
