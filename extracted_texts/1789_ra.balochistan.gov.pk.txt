What we do!
Our Services
Services
Solutions
The Religious Affairs Department of Balochistan plays a pivotal role in managing religious matters and fostering interfaith harmony. Our key responsibilities include organizing Hajj and Umrah services, maintaining religious institutions, and ensuring the peaceful observance of religious festivals and events.
Key Functions:
Facilitation of Hajj and Umrah services
Coordination of religious education and institutions
Promotion of interfaith harmony and religious tolerance
Overseeing mosque and madrassa affairs
Supervising religious festivals and observances
Addressing religious grievances and conflict resolution
