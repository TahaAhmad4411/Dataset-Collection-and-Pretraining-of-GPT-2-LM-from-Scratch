Honorable Chief Court Judges Official Tour to Chilas for Division Bench Cases
06 Jun 2024
The Hon'ble Judges of Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Court, Mr Justice Raja Shakeel Ahmed, Judge and Mr Justice Mushtaq Muhammad, Judge are proceeding to Chilas on an official tour to hear Division Bench and Single Bench cases pertaining to Diamer Division from 12th June 2024 to 15th June 2024.
The concerned quarters are requested to arrange security arrangements for the Hon'ble Judges of Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Court during the travel and stay at Chilas.

Furthermore, the Chief Engineer Water & Power Diamer is directed to ensure the uninterrupted electricity supply at the Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Court Circuit Bench/Registry Chilas and Residence of the Hon'ble Judges during the official tour. For further details please find the attached document.
