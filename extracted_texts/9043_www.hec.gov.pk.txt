Page Content
​​​​The Higher Education Commission, since its inception, has adopted a proactive policy of encouraging the sponsors/management to establish/strengthen universities/institutions to make them high quality seats of learning.
The general
SOPs for Establishment of a New University / Institution of Higher Education
have been devised after the approval of the Executive Body of HEC viz. Commission.
The comprehensive parameters/facilities required to be fulfilled prior to establishment of a new university/institution has been approved by the Federal Cabinet in February, 2002 and assented by the Chancellors' Committee headed by the President of Pakistan.
For establishing a new University or seat of higher learning under charters by respective governments, the sponsors are required to prepare a comprehensive Feasibility Report based on the parameters given in the attached booklet namely
Criteria/Guidelines for the Establishment of a New University/Institute of Higher Education
A model charter has also been approved for effective management of universities/ institutions namely
Federal Universities Ordinance, 2002
.

Affiliation Criteria​
has been specifically devised for Public Sector Universities/ Institutions
