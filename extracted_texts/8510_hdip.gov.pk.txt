Basin Studies
HDIP combines high-level expertise with well-equipped sophisticated laboratory facilities to help upstream and downstream petroleum sector. Basin analysis and 1D Modeling Sedimentology, Geophysics, Geochemistry, Petroleum Engineering, Biostratigraphy, Crude Oil Evaluation and Testing of Petroleum Products.
GEOCHEMISTRY
HDIP’s well equipped laboratories provide comprehensive, cost-effective analytical services in petroleum geochemistry. Our professional staff have over 25 years experience in petroleum geochemistry and other geochemical studies and are considered specialists in their field. We provide a complete analytical service in organic geochemistry for petroleum exploration and production companies. These services extend to environmental monitoring and the determination various contaminants in soil and water.
The geochemistry service includes:
A complete and comprehensive geochemical service, which includes sample tracking, sample preparation, analysis, interpretation and reporting.
Interpretation and project review, planning
Specialized analytical service (GC-MS etc.)
Reservoir Oil Fingerprinting (continuity studies)
Surface Geochemistry

HDIP
The HDIP is the national Petroleum Research & Development (R&D) organization. The objective of HDIP is to undertake, promote and guide scientific research and development in the field of petroleum in upstream and downstream and for matters connected therewith.
