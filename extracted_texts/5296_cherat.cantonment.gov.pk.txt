DGML&C shared his vision of new reforms in CB education system.
To revive the glorious traditions of imparting quality education through revamped existing CB Education System by developing modern, competitive, self-sustaining, vibrant and dignified education institutions with futuristic approach to serve even better the residents of Cantonments in Pakistan.
