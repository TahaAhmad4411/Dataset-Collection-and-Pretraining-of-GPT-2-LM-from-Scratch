OVERVIEW
The AJK food Authority has been working as a functional 
                    entity across all 10 districts since July 2012. 
                    Specialized wings “Resource & Licensing”, “Technical” and “Operations” 
                    which are functional and working to enforce the food hygiene and quality 
                    standards as described in the AJK Food Authority Act 2011 and the Pure 
                    Food Rules 2011 is carried out through qualified teams of Food Safety 
                    Officers (FSOs’) and Assistant Food Safety Officers (AFSOs’). Food Safety 
                    teams ensures the food safety.
