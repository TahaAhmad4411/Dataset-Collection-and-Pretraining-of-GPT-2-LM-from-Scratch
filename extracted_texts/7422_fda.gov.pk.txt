Introduction
Engineering directorate is one of the prime directorates of FDA, Faisalabad which is responsible for planning, design and execution of development-related projects i.e.  roads and buildings sectors in Faisalabad city.
Function
Planning and design of proposed projects under various programs, i.e., ADP, SAP, CDP funded by Govt.
To resolve the traffic conflicts on various roads in Faisalabad city by planning, design and execution of underpasses, bridges etc.
The planning and design of the engineering works of various housing colonies, e.g., Millat Town, Medina Town, Gulshan colony etc. which are under the jurisdiction of FDA.
Development related works of FDA city housing scheme.
Repair and maintenance of office and residential buildings of FDA.
