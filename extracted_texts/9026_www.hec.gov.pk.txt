Click here to download User manual for Electric Wheelchair
Prospective students would fill in their particulars via online application forms on a given link at HEC website. The form would be printed, necessary documents attached and submitted through office of the respective Vice Chancellor or designated person at the university/Degree Awarding Institute.
Universities will forward all eligible cases to HEC for distribution of wheel chairs.
Verification of Applications:

he Applications received would be forwarded through the Vice Chancellors' office and would be accompanied by a medical certificate satisfying the verification criteria. ​
