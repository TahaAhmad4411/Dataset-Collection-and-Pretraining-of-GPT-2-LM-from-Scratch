Chairman CDA Muhammad Ali Randhawa visit Jinnah Convention Center
Islamabad: 24th July, 2024: On the instructions of the Prime Minister of
                Pakistan and the Interior Minister, Chairman CDA Muhammad Ali Randhawa visited the Jinnah Convention
                Center today along with senior officials of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The purpose of the visit
                was to strategize the renovation of the center to bring it up to international standards, making it a
                prominent venue for global events and conferences. The initiative will highlight Islamabad's commitment
                to improving its infrastructure and promoting it as a hub for international diplomacy and events.
Together, let's shape a dynamic and sustainable future for
                                Islamabad,
                                where every resident's well-being is our priority.
