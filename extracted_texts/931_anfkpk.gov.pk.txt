Recovered 24 Kgs of Charas 12 Kgs Opium from Trunk of a Car
Anti Narcotics Force KPK, on a tip-off, intercepted a Car, arrested an accused namly Imtiaz Akhtar (r/o District Lakki Marwat) and recovered 20x pkts of Charas total weighing 24 kgs(1200 gm each) & 10x pkts of Opium total weighing 12 kgs(1200 gm each) which were laying in the Trunk of the car. The recovered narco was being smuggled from Peshawar to Punjab.
Case has been registered under CNS Act and further investigation is in process.
