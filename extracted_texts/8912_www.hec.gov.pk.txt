Eligibility Criteria
Page Content
​Afghan national with strong academic background are encouraged to apply. The applicants must fulfill the following eligibility criteria:
Undergraduate:
Applicants should have completed 12 years or equivalent education.
The age limit is 17-23 years before closing date of application.
60 % Marks for general
subjects as per IBCC Pakistan equivalency formula.
65% Medical & Engineering subjects
​
​MS/M.Phil
35 Years maximum age limit at date of applications closing.
​Candidates must have completed 16 years of education.
Minimum 2.5 CGPA or equivalent out of 4.0 is required in Bachelor's/Mater's degree (16 years of education)
​PhD:
The age limit is 35 years for non-faculty members and 40 years of faculty members.
Candidates must have 18 years of education.
​A minimum 3.0 CGPA out of 4.0 is required in MS/MPhil degree.
