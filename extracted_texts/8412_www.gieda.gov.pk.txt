Biometric System Mandated for Balochistan Government Offices
The chief Minister of Balochistan, Mir Sarfaraz Bugti, along with the Chief Secretary, Has ordered the installation of a Biometric system in all Government offices across the province. This measure is intended to ensure that employees adhere to their duties, thereby improving the timely resolution of Public issues. Additionally, all officers and employees in Gieda have been instructed to comply with this directive.
