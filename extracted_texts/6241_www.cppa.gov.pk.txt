Contract Signing Ceremony of ERP Implementation Phase II
2018-04-19
The contract for ERP implementation Phase II has been awarded to M/s Carbon8 (Pvt.) Ltd. On the Contract Signing Ceremony, Mr. Abid Latif Lodhi (CEO CPPA-G) appreciated the tremendous efforts of CPPA-G Management and users on successful implementation of ERP Phase I. He also expressed his best wishes for the success of ERP implementation Phase II and ensured his full commitment and support to the M/s Carbon8 and CPPA-G Project Team to make this project also another success story.
Mr. Jawad Ayub (CEO Carbon8) along with his management representative attended the event. Mr. Rihan Akhtar (CFO CPPA-G), Syed Iqbal Mehdi (CTO CPPA-G), Mr. Arshad Javed Minhas (CIO CPPA-G), Mr. Muhammad Shabbir (DGMF) and Mr. Namet Ullah (Manager ERP) were also present at this memorable event.
