Enforcement Directorate
The enforcement directorate is entrusted with a critical role in the capital area, encompassing various key responsibilities:
Removal of Encroachments: The directorate is responsible for identifying and addressing encroachments on public and designated areas. This involves the removal of structures, possessions, or activities that encroach upon spaces not intended for such use.
Combatting Illegal Constructions: It plays a vital role in monitoring and taking action against illegal constructions. This includes structures that are built without proper permits or in violation of zoning and building regulations.
Clearance for Developmental Work: The enforcement directorate ensures that areas designated for developmental projects are cleared of any obstructions or encroachments that may hinder such projects. This is critical for the successful execution of infrastructure and development initiatives.
Overall, the enforcement directorate's efforts are integral to maintaining order, legality, and the unimpeded progress of developmental work in the capital area. Their work contributes to urban planning, safety, and the preservation of public spaces.

Overview of Master Plan
Federal Capital Commission was established in 1959 to prepare master plan for new capital.
Doxiadis associates were appointed as consultant of this commission.
The master plan was approved by federal cabinet on 26th October, 1960.
In 1963, area of the capital site was declared 906.5 Sq.kms.
CDA was established in 1960 to develop the city as per master plan.
City is planned on the concept of dynapolis and planned on grid-iron pattern dividing the city neighborhoods called sectors.
Each sector is an independent neighborhood community measuring 6300’X6300’.
In 1992, division of Islamabad into 5 zones.
In 2010, zone-4 was further subdivided into 4 sub-Zones.
As per census 2017, population of Islamabad is 2,006,572 (49.42% Rural, 50.58% Urban). Present population of the capital is estimated to 2.3 million.
