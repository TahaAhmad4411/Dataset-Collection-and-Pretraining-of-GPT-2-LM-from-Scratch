Honorable Chief Justice Approves Circuit Bench Roster for Diamer and Baltistan Divisions
31 Jul 2023
In exercise of the powers conferred under Article-89(2) of the Government of the Gilgit-Baltistan Order 2018, the Hon'ble Chief Justice Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Court, Gilgit has been pleased to approve the roster sitting in respect of the following Hon'ble Judges of Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Court, at Circuit bench Diamer Division and Baltistan Division from 01-8-2023 to 30-09-2023 in the interest of litigant public as mentioned in attached document.
