About Mines & Minerals Department Gilgit Baltistan
Vision
Bring Economic Prosperity in the region through Development of Mineral Potential of Gilgit Baltistan as well as to Promote Mineral Business activities by creating an economic & encouraging environment for public & private sector investment in Gilgit Balti
Mission
To strive for social development and economic prosperity of people, especially the poor and under privileged masses, -To provide opportunities for large scale employment through private sector. To develop human capital to meet the needs of industry

Messages
It is my privilege to serve as the Secretary of this crucial department, dedicated to ensuring the development of this much neglected sector. Our department is committed to modernize and enhance efficiency and bring transparency in addition to implement the ease of doing business policy of the government.
The mineral sector of Gilgit Baltistan has the potential to become one of the leading sectors in a very short span of time.  Nature has endowed this area with an estimated One Trillion Dollars  worth of minerals in addition to unparallel natural beauty.
We are striving to explore mineral potential of Gilgit-Baltistan in line with international standards.
Simultaneously, this department is also responsible for development of industry, commerce and labor sectors.
The efforts are ongoing to develop all these sectors, bring investment, generate jobs and enhance the standard of living of the people of this area, to the best of my ability.

Law & Order
Is it necessary to obtain an NOC only from the mining department to obtain a lease?
NOCs from Forest Department, Wildlife Department and Departments related to Environmental proctection is necessary. Without NOCs no lease will be granted to the leasee.
How much time is required to grant a license or lease?
It depends on the concerned company or applicant. If they submit the required documents within a month, the department issues the lease license within that month.
