Recovery of Ice Heroin from Bahrain Bound Passengers at Islamabad and Faisalabad Airports
ASF personnel recovered 1.046 kg of Ice Heroin from Bahrain bound passenger at Faisalabad airport and 0.726 kg of Ice Heroin from Bahrain bound passenger at Islamabad airport.
Passenger Mohammad Ibrahim travelling from Faisalabad Airport skillfully hid ice heroin in a bag - however, ASF personnel foiled the smuggling attempts by recovering drugs during the search of the luggage.

Passenger Abu Zar travelling from Islamabad Airport skillfully hid ice heroin in a bag - however, ASF personnel foiled the smuggling attempts by recovering drugs during the search of the luggage.
After initial investigation, the accused were handed over to the ANF authorities for further legal action along with the exported drugs.

ASF, an executive department of Ministry of Aviation was established in 1976 under the Airports Security Force Act LXXVII of 1975. 
                            ASF has been mandated with the task of providing security to all Civil Aviation Airports of Pakistan. Currently ASF is securing 25 operational 
                            Airports (12 International and 13 Domestic Airports). ASF ensures security of Aircrafts, Passengers, their luggage, mail along with proper 
                            conduct of persons and vehicles within the limits of airports and aerodromes.
