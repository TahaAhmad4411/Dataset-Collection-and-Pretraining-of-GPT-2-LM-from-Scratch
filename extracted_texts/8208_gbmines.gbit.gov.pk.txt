About
About Us:
Mines & Minerals GB is a pivotal entity in the mining industry of Gilgit Baltistan (GB), known for its significant contribution to the region's economy through the extraction and processing of mineral resources. The department plays a crucial role in harnessing the mineral wealth of the area, ensuring sustainable utilization while adhering to environmental regulations. With a focus on responsible mining practices, Mines & Minerals GB strives to foster growth and development in the region by creating employment opportunities and supporting local communities. The organization's commitment to operational excellence and safety standards has positioned it as a reputable player in the mining sector, contributing to the overall prosperity and growth of Gilgit Baltistan.
Vision:
Bring Economic Prosperity in the region through Development of Mineral Potential of Gilgit Baltistan as well as to Promote Mineral Business activities by creating an economic & encouraging environment for public & private sector investment in Gilgit Baltistan
Mission:
Our mission is to drive economic prosperity in Gilgit Baltistan by fostering the development of the mineral sector. We aim to create a supportive and encouraging environment for both public and private sector investments, promoting sustainable mineral business activities that contribute to regional growth and community well-being. Through collaboration and innovation, we strive to unlock the full potential of our mineral resources for the benefit of all stakeholders.
