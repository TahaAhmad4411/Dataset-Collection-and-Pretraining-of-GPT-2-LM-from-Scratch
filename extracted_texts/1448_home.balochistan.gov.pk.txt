About Us
The
Home and Tribal Affairs Department
is a key pillar of the Government of Balochistan, tasked with maintaining law and order, managing tribal relations, and ensuring public safety across the province. The department plays a critical role in supporting law enforcement agencies, mediating tribal conflicts, and coordinating efforts to respond to natural disasters.
Balochistan is home to a rich cultural heritage with a complex tribal structure. Managing relationships between these communities is vital to ensuring peace and security. Through strong leadership, the department coordinates with tribal leaders, law enforcement, and various provincial agencies to foster an environment of trust, cooperation, and peaceful resolution of disputes.
The department is also committed to developing policies and programs that protect citizens, prevent crime, and prepare communities for disasters, working closely with relevant stakeholders to enhance public safety.
