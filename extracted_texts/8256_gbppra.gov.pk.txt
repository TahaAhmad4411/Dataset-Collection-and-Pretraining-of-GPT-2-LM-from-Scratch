Rules and Regulations
Welcome to the Rules and Regulations section of the Public Procurement Regulatory Authority (PPRA), Gilgit Baltistan. Our commitment to transparency, fairness, and efficiency in public procurement is reflected in the detailed guidelines outlined in this section. These rules serve as the cornerstone for governing procurement processes, ensuring accountability, and fostering a competitive and ethical environment.
Key Highlights:
1. Transparency and Accountability
Our rules emphasize transparency throughout the procurement lifecycle. From planning and tendering to contract awarding, these guidelines are designed to provide clear procedures, promoting openness and accountability in every stage of the procurement process.
2. Fair Competition
Ensuring fair competition is a core principle of our regulations. By establishing equitable and non-discriminatory practices, we create a level playing field for suppliers, encouraging healthy competition and optimizing value for money in public procurement.
3. Ethical Conduct
Ethical behavior is paramount in public procurement. Our regulations set high standards for ethical conduct, guiding both government entities and suppliers to uphold integrity, honesty, and professionalism in all interactions related to procurement activities.
4. Efficiency and Effectiveness
To enhance efficiency, our rules incorporate best practices for procurement planning, evaluation, and decision-making. By streamlining processes, we aim to expedite procurement cycles while maintaining the highest standards of quality and compliance.
5. Accessibility
We believe in making our rules and regulations easily accessible to all stakeholders. Whether you are a government entity, supplier, or a concerned citizen, this section provides comprehensive insights into the guidelines governing public procurement in Gilgit Baltistan.
Feel free to explore the detailed rules and regulations outlined below. If you have any questions or need further clarification, do not hesitate to reach out to our dedicated team. Together, let's build a procurement landscape characterized by transparency, fairness, and efficiency.
