On April 16, 2024, Dr. Razia Fatima chaired the Consultative Meeting for the Revision of National TB Management Guidelines. The process engaged in provincial consultations and culminated in a final review in Islamabad. The collaborative effort aims to enhance the effectiveness of “Revised National TB Management Guidelines 2024.”

CMU under the leadership of the Ministry of National Health Services Regulations & Coordination and in close collaboration with technical and development partners is organizing cascade of activities for the preparation & submission of the Country’s proposal for the next GF funding cycle for Malaria ( 2024-2026).
