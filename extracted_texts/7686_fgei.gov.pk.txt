Schools
Schools are the foremost foundation of knowledge and learning, providing the essential start needed for intellectual, mental, physical, social, ethical, inspirational, and behavioral development in young minds.
The FGEI Directorate recognizes the vital role of schools in our nation’s growth. With a network of 311 schools across Pakistan, ranging from Primary to High and Higher Secondary levels, it is committed to providing quality education. The administration and faculty of these institutions diligently shoulder their responsibilities, nurturing dynamic young minds to become valuable contributors to the country’s progress.
