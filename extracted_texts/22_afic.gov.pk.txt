15 Annual ECG Workshop
Previous image
Next image
It is my pleasure to invite you to participate in ECG Workshop on 21 October 2023 at Armed Forces Institute of Cardiology, Rawalpindi. This event is being organized in collaboration with Pakistan Heart Rhythm Society.
The workshop shall showcase sessions encompassing an array of heart rhythm disorders. It will cater to the needs of doctors ranging from medical students to Cardiac Electrophysiologists. One of the highlights of the workshop shall be interactive ECG sessions designed specifically for our junior colleagues. The workshop is expected to be attended by eminent Cardiac Electrophysiologists of Pakistan. I hope you will have an educationally satisfying experience.
Looking forward to welcome you.
