Distribution of Sports Kits in School / Colleges of FDE by Parliamentary Secretary
Today Honorable Parliamentary Secretary Ms Zeb Jaffar has distributed sports kits in Schools / College of FDE. During this month she has committed to distribute 30 sports kits in various schools which will expanded to remaining institutes in next financial year.
