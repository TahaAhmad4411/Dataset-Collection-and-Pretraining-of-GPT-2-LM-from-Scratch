CSD has introduced Home 51 package for Armed Forces and entitled clientele. You can purchase household items like furniture, crockery, geysers, motorcycles etc and used cars in your entitled limits.CSD is striving hard to provide the best possible svcs, through its HP facility to pers of Armed Forces. HP facility encompasses provision of house hold/elect items, Motor Cycles and Car/Tractors on soft terms/conditions bank/leasing agencies.
