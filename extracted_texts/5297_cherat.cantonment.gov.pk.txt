TIP Tax (Transfer of immoveable property Tax)
On Transfer of immovable property, TIP Tax is charged at the rate of 3% of the consideration value of the property as per approved STR Rates
If TIP Tax is not paid before registering the sale deed, 10% extra fee is charged on total value the property
Concession in payment of TIP Tax
No TIP Tax is charged on transfer of immovable property in case of gift deeds only from parents to their children
