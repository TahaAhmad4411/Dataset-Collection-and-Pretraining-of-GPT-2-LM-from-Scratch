Undergraduate Scholarships Programme for the Students of Gilgit-Baltistan
​​​
Gilgit-Baltistan has significant geographical importance and is the terminal point of CPEC. The main aim of this program is to provide financial assistance to GB’s students aspiring for undergraduate studies in the best Public Sector Universities in Pakistan. The program is also in line with the Sustainable Development Goals (SGDs) of Pakistan i.e. To eradicate extreme poverty and mobilize resources; therefore, to accomplish these goals, the government has directed HEC to expand higher education scholarships for the least developed areas and reduce inequality within the country.
