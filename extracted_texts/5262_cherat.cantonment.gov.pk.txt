Bazzar Committee is a Sub-Committee of the Cantonment Board constituted U/S 43-A of the Cantonments Act, 1924 to deal the following applications belongs to properties fall inside Bazar area: -
Applications received U/S 179 of the Cantonments Act, 1924 for seeking approval of the Board for erection / re-erection of residential and commercial buildings.
Applications received U/S 183-A of the Cantonments Act, 1924 for grant of extension in time limit for completion of residential and commercial buildings.
Applications received U/S 73 of the Cantonments Act, 1924 for mutation of properties.
