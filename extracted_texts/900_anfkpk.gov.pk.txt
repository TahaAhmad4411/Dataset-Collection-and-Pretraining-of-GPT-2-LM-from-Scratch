Anti Narcotics Force KP seized Heroin at BKIAP
Anti Narcotics Force (ANF) Peshawar at Bacha Khan International Airport (BKIAP) confiscated a suspected consignment of Surgical equipment, booked for USA via UK.
11.950 KGs of Heroine was recovered from above mentioned consignment.
Case has been registered, and further investigation in progress.
