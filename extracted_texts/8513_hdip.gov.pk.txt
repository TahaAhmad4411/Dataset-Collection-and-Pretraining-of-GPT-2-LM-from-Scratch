CNG Sector Training
Hydrocarbon Development Institute of Pakistan (HDIP) offers three comprehensive courses in the field of Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) for Compressor Operator, Refueling Attendant and Certification of CNG system in Automobile. These courses are designed to focus on the CNG Rules, 1992 for safe installation, handling and operation of CNG equipment. The courses are conducted and supervised by experienced and Professional Engineers.

HDIP
The HDIP is the national Petroleum Research & Development (R&D) organization. The objective of HDIP is to undertake, promote and guide scientific research and development in the field of petroleum in upstream and downstream and for matters connected therewith.
