Faisalabad Development Authority
Faisalabad Development Authority, Faisalabad was constituted under Punjab Development of Cities Act 1976 as a successor body to Lyallpur Improvement Trust (1966).Faisalabad Development Authority (FDA) is a body responsible for undertaking and monitoring planned developments in the city of Faisalabad, in Punjab, Pakistan. The body acts as a regulatory authority for overseeing the construction of houses, commercial developments and residential areas in the city.
As the Head of Faisalabad Development Authority, I want to extend my warmest greetings to all of you. Our city has been growing rapidly in recent years, and it's our goal to ensure that this growth is sustainable, inclusive, and meets the needs of all of our residents.We are committed to providing high-quality infrastructure, housing, and public services to all of our citizens. We believe that by working together, we can build a brighter future for Faisalabad, one that is prosperous, safe, and welcoming to all.
Muhammad Asif Ch.
