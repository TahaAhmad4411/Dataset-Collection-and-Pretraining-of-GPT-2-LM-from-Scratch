Professional Training of Sri Lankan Government Officials
Page Content
​Recognizing the requirement of capacity building of Civil Servants of Srilankan government, Pak-Sri Lanka Higher Education Cooperation Programme offers training of Srilankan government officials. The primary focus of this activity is to provide management training at middle and senior level positions. Srilankan government will nominate 10 government officials each year for a comprehensive training in Islamabad. The trainin​g will be offered in the following areas:
Public Sector Management & Governance
Human Resource Management
Public Policy Planning & Implementation
Office Management
Financial Management, Budget Making, and Audit
Project Planning & Management,
Monitoring and Evaluation
Record Management
Presentation Skills, Communication Skills
Information Technology Skills
Handling of official papers (secrecy etc.)
Strategic Management
Social, Political and Economic System
Local Government System
Civil and Criminal Laws
Morality, Ethics & Accountability in Public Service​
