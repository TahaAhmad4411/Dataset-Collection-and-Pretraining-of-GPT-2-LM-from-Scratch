Hearing Of DB Cases at Registry Office Skardu Will Be Start From 8th September 2022
08 Sep 2022
The division bench of the Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Court consisting of the Hon'ble Mr. Justice Ali Baig, Chief Justice, and Hon'ble Mr. Justice Raja Shakeel Ahmed, Judge is proceeding to Skardu on 7th September 2022 for hearing of Division/Single bench cases pertaining to Baltistan Region at Registry Office Skardu from 08th September 2022 to 10th September 2022. This issues with the approval of the Hon'ble Chief Justice.
