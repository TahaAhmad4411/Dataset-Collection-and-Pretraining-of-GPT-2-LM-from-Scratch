Mr. Asim Akram, Honourable Member
Mr. Asim Akram, Honourable Member
Honorable Member
Mr. Asim Akram
was born on 26
th
March 1973. He received his matriculation from Shaheen Intermediate College, F-6, R.F. Kamra. Later on, he moved to Lahore and received his further education from there. He secured his decree in law (LLB) from University of the Punjab.
Honorable Member
Mr. Asim Akram
was enrolled as Advocate lower courts in October, 1997 and as Advocate High Court in October, 1999. He established his own law firm
Asim
&
Associate
at Lahore in 2000. He has twenty-five years of experience as an active lawyer in diversified fields mainly civil, constitutional and service matters to his credit.
Honorable Member
Mr. Asim Akram
was appointed as
member of Federal Service Tribunal in November, 2021.
