NON TEACHING STAFF (NTS)
AIM
Our aim is to make our organization a place of excellence where employees can achieve full potential in their personal, moral and career development.
OBJECTIVES
We are responsible for policy making & overall supervision, control and administration of non-teaching staff.
We timely & effectively resolve issues and service related matters of NTS on merits with empathy.
We strive to provide healthy hygienic working conditions, standard working hours, promotions leading to satisfaction of our staff.
We are providing quality services, welfare facilities & social security to non-teaching staff.
We develop policies & procedures that encourages employee’s growth & development by providing them opportunities for learning and trainings to increase their capabilities.
We maintain the productivity of our staff by creating environment where employees feel valued, motivated & engaged in their work. We give our employees a variety of tasks related to:-
Accounts
Office management
Administration
IT
Legal Matters
We can post our employees anywhere in Pakistan while giving you opportunity to become adaptable in different situations and culture.
We are creating a culture of innovation, collaboration and trust that benefit employees and organization to enhance the retention of staff, increase productivity & improve organizational reputation as employer of choice.
KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
Promotions
Are you looking for an employment opportunity where you can have career growth opportunities time to time?  FGEIs is the right place because we know when it comes to motivating / encouraging employee’s promotions play a vital role. We are delighted to let you know that we provide you promotion opportunities purely on merit up to BPS-19.
87 x promotions have been awarded in 2023/24.
Transfer / Postings
For any department postings play critical role in efficient management of HR. Transfer / Posting offer career development opportunities by exposure to new areas. We consider transfer / posting requests regularly on humanitarian grounds.
1183 x such postings have been made in 2023/24.
Employees Welfare
Welfare of employees come first. When employees feel that their welfare is a top priority in their organization, they feel motivated, committed and passionate towards their work. We have dedicated staff to deal with following welfare cases related to benefit of employees on daily basis:-
Motorcycle / Car & House Building & GP Fund Advances
Departmental Permission Certificates & NOCs
Pension / Family Pension
Benevolent Fund LPR/ Encashment
Leave Cases
Marriage Grants
Exemption of Funds
Mortgage / De-Mortgage
Farewell Grant
A total of 1119 x welfare cases has been finalized in 2023/24.
