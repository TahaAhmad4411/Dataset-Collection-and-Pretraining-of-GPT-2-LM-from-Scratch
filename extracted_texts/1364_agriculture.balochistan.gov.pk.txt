Mandate
To provide advisory service with regard to crop production, protection, Soil health and Agriculture marketing through its wide spread extension network.
To bridge gap between farmers and Agriculture Research.
To regulate supply of inputs like seed, fertilizer and pesticides.
To promote and enhance agriculture productivity through Extension service.
Set up of Agriculture Extension (Excluding OFWM)
Directorate General at provincial level with supporting Directorates of Adaptive Research, information, Planning, Plant Protection, Procurement and Supplies, Floriculture, Seed, Farms, In-Service Academy,  Soil Fertility and Economics & Marketing.
Divisional Directors at Divisional level.
Deputy Director of Extension, Soil Fertility and Plant Protection etc at District level.
Agriculture Officers at tehsil level.
Field Assistant and Belders at
