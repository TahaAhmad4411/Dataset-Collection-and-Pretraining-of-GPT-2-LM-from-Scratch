INITIAL PROPERTY VERIFICATION SERVICE (IPVS)
IPVS is a groundbreaking solution designed to provide you with
                        unparalleled access to comprehensive and reliable property information. It serves as your
                        gateway to streamlined property verification.
We offer an extensive and regularly updated property database with diverse
                        options. Our team ensures thorough verification, reviewing legal documents and property history
                        for accuracy. Our user-friendly platform, IPVS, makes it easy to explore properties, access
                        verified data, and make well-informed decisions effortlessly.
The primary notion behind the launch of this service is to facilitate
                        overseas Pakistanis, who are considered, “The supreme fortune of Pakistan”. Property
                        verification while purchasing a property in any housing scheme, vertical or commercial project,
                        has been made possible through ingenious efforts ofCDA. This service renders Property
                        Verification Report (PVR) which is publicly available and bring forth the following information:

Your 6-Step Guide to CDA Inital Property
                            Verification Service
Register for IPVS (Initial Property Verification Service)
Provide the required details to create your IPVS account.
An email for verification will be sent to your provided email address.
Click on the address provided in the email to successfully proceed with account creation.
Login with Credentials
Access your IPVS account securely by logging in with the credentials you used during
                            registration.
Input Property Details
Select the desired address or property you wish to verify using the search field, or zoom
                            into the map to selection of property/address.
Select Your Service
Choose the specific service you need from the left side menu.
Request Initial Property Verification Report (OPVR)
Click on the button “Get your Initial Property Verification Report (OPVR)” to proceed with
                            the request.
A success message will be shown confirming the submission of your request.
Instant Report Delivery
Your comprehensive Initial Property Verification Report will be delivered electronically to
                            your account.
