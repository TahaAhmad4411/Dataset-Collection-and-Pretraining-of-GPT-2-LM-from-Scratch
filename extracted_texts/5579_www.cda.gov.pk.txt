Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi decides to transforming Islamabad into a world-class city
To reduce environmental pollution, CDA will purchase only electric bikes from now on
                Eco-friendly electric buses will be operated for the citizens of Islamabad Interior Minister Mohsin
                Naqvi banned the purchase of petrol motorcycles International standard emergency services will be
                provided to the citizens of Islamabad. Mohsin Naqvi Decision to transfer emergency services from MCI to
                CDA The meeting also focused on progress made in projects including the refurbishment of the Federal
                Secretariat, Diplomatic Enclave enhancements, and the introduction of smart parking solutions. CDA
                Chairman Muhammad Ali Randhawa provided a detailed briefing on these initiatives. Naqvi emphasized
                transforming Islamabad into a world-class city, including environmental measures like only purchasing
                electric bikes and introducing eco-friendly electric buses for public transport. In addition,
                international standard emergency services will be provided by transferring responsibilities from the
                Metropolitan Corporation Islamabad (MCI) to the CDA. The establishment of a new Capital Water and
                Sanitation Agency (Capital WASA) is also underway. Naqvi announced that construction work on the Serena
                Chowk underpass and F-9 Park Chowk flyover projects would commence soon. Long-delayed developments in
                non-developed sectors of Islamabad will also be expedited. The Islamabad Model Jail project, which has
                been stalled for 17 years, is progressing swiftly, with the first phase to be completed on schedule. The
                meeting concluded with Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi congratulating the CDA team for record tax
                collection and stressing the need to enhance institutional assets beyond selling plots and land.
