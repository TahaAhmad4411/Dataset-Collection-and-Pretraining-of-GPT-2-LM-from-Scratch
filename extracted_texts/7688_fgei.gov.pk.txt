Uniform Guidelines
Following approved uniform pattern will be worn by all college students w.e.f August 15, 2018:
Boys
Class wise colour classification is as under:
1st Year - Blue Badge dia with College Monogram
2nd Year - White Badge dia with College Monogram
3rd Year - Grey Badge dia with College Monogram
4th Year - Green Badge dia with College Monogram
Boys Summer Dress (1st Shift)
Trouser - Dark Grey
Shirt - White
Shoes - Oxford
Socks - Black/ Grey
Boys – Summer Dress (2nd Shift)
Trouser - Dark Grey (As already in use)
Shirt - Light Blue
Shoes - Oxford
Socks - Black/ Grey
Boys Winter Dress
Classification of class wise identification (As per above). However 1 to 4 white satin strips on coat arm end from 1st year to 4th year be stitched.
Girls
Girls Summer Dress (1st Shift)
Shalwar - White
Qameez - White with Navy Blue strips
Dupatta - Navy Blue with White border
Shoes - Coat Shoes
Socks - White
Girls Summer Dress (2nd Shift)
Shalwar - White
Qameez - White with Turquoise (Ferozi) strips
Dupatta - Turquoise (Ferozi) with White border
Shoes - Coat Shoes
Socks - White
Girls Winter
(Same as above with following additions)
Jersey - Black
Coat - Navy Blue
