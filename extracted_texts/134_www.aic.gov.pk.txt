Program & Media Cell
Programme and Media Cell was established in 2010 first time in the Aiwan-e-Iqbal Complex. Earlier, no attention was given towards such platform. The main objective of this cell is to propagate, spread and promote the vision, thought and message of Doctor Allama Muhammad Iqbal in the professional way.
Programme and Media Cell to lay special focuses on spreading message of Iqbal through seminars, scholar’s meetings, monthly Tafheem-e-Iqbal lectures, Iqbal aur Tasawuf lectures, Iqbal and Iqbaliyat, Regular visits of students and other departments, Summer Camp “Iqbal kay Shaheen”.
