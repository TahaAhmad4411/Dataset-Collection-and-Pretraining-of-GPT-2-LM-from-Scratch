Minorities Consultative Conferences
5th Jan 2023
A Series of Minorities Consultative Conferences by AIMH
The presence of minorities in a state not only represents its religious and cultural diversity, but it also impacts upon national, ethnic, and religious integrity of the country, where minority as well as majority members promote values of peace and interfaith harmony. This in view, two special consultative conferences were held on 5th and 26th January 2023.
Purpose and Objectives of the Conference
The conferences were aimed at promoting unity and cohesiveness amongst different religious groups, besides providing a platform for open dialogue on topics crucial to fostering interfaith harmony. It also sought ideas and proposals for peaceful coexistence in society from community representatives. The event also acknowledged the role of Sparkling Whites of Pakistan in national integration and progress, and sacrifices rendered by them during peace and war, in the history of Pakistan.
