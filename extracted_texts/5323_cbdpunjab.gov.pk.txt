PCBDDA HONORS WOMEN’S EMPOWERMENT ON INTERNATIONAL WOMEN'S DAY
Punjab Central Business District Development Authority (PCBDDA), known as Central Business District Punjab (CBD Punjab, commemorated International Women's Day at CBD Punjab House.
Punjab Central Business District Development Authority (PCBDDA), known as Central Business District Punjab (CBD Punjab, commemorated International Women's Day at CBD Punjab House. The ceremony aimed to honor and inspire women in all aspects of life. This year's theme, "Innovation and Technology for Gender Equality," highlighted the pivotal role of technology in bridging the gender gap and promoting equal opportunities.
The event witnessed the presence of COO Brigadier (R) Mansoor Janjua, Directorate Heads of CBD Punjab, and the dedicated female staff members. Esteemed speakers emphasized the significance of gender equality in the workplace and acknowledged the invaluable contributions of women to society.
Expressing his viewpoint, COO Brigadier (R) Mansoor Janjua stated, "We firmly believe that women are integral to our society, and their contributions are priceless. At CBD Punjab, we are committed to providing equal opportunities and steadfast support for their growth and development. Our religion, Islam, has bestowed equal rights upon women, recognizing them as respected members of society."
The female staff members of CBD Punjab were also recognized for their unwavering dedication and presented with certificates of appreciation. This gesture conveyed gratitude from the CBD Punjab management for their significant contributions to the authority. The certificates were presented by the COO himself.
The celebration served as a poignant reminder that women play a vital role in building a prosperous society. PCBDDA remains committed to fostering an inclusive, diverse, and supportive workplace for all employees, irrespective of gender.
