Job Description
A Construction Helper is responsible for providing assistance in various construction tasks on construction sites. Their job duties may include carrying and setting up materials and tools, moving heavy equipment, cleaning and preparing sites, as well as assisting tradesmen and other construction professionals. They may also be responsible for performing basic carpentry, plumbing or electrical work, as well as helping with concrete pouring and finishing, demolition work or excavation.
