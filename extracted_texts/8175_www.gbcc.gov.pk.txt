Gilgit-Baltistan Courts to Remain Closed for Eid-ul-Azha
27 Jun 2023
The Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Court & its subordinate Courts of Gilgit-Baltistan Judiciary will remain closed with effect from 28th June 2023 to 1 July 2023 on account of Eid-ul-Azha. The District & Sessions Judges are requested to inform the subordinate Courts under their control. This Issue with the approval of the Hon'ble Chief Justice.
