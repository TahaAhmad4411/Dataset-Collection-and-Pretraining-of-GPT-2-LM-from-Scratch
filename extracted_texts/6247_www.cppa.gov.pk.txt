Go-Live of Oracle Financials ERP in CPPA inaugurated by Mr. M. Zargham (Member BOD) on 14-09-2017
2017-09-14
Oracle ERP Financials System at CPPA is successfully implemented and Go Live on 11-09-2017. The formal inauguration ceremony was held on 14-09-2017. On this occasion Mr. Muhammad Zargham (Member BOD), Mr. Abid Latif Lodhi (CEO), Mr. Muhammad Shabbir (CFO/ ERP Project Director), Mr. Rehan Hameed (DG HR&A), Mr. Arshad Minhas (CIO), Mr. Namet Ullah (Manager ERP/ Project Manager), other management of CPPA and Staff members of Accounts & Finance department were also present at this memorable event.
