Physical History Preservation and Education Programme
To locate, identify, examine and document, catalogue and facilitate preservation of Pakistan’s rich physical (military) history from ancient times to the present, including battlefields, historical routes, monuments and war graves.
Based on Physical History Preservation and Education Programme, to arrange military staff rides and guided tours to identified sites, for both civil and military groups, so as to promote a better understanding of our military history.

The Army Institute of Military History
Preserve and promote the military history of Pakistan and the history of the Pakistan Army, while acting as centre of excellence for studying military history, and providing a forum for open-source intellectual input to the Pakistan Army, through two-way interaction with academia and civil society
