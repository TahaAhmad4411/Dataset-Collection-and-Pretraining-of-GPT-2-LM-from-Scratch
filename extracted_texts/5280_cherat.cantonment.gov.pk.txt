OVERVIEW
Chaklala Cantonment Board (CCB) is an autonomous body working under Military Lands and Cantonment Department, Ministry of Defence, Government of Pakistan. With ever increasing population of the cantonment, CCB was established on 1st February 2003 upon bifurcation of Rawalpindi Cantonment into two Cantts i.e. Chaklala Cantonment and Rawalpindi Cantonment. CCB aims at providing high-quality services with access to all which can make a decent living place to live in and to leave a sustainable world for future generations.
