Page Content
​Following are resources and services of HEC Library:
Digital Library Services.
Shifting of PhD theses to National Library of Pakistan (NLP).
Current Content Services (CCS).
Preservation of newspapers up to 01 year.
Preservation of back-dated volumes of journals.
Cataloguing in Publication (CIP).
HEC Recognised Journals Identification from Printed and Online Indexes/Abstracts i.e. Ulrich periodical dictionary, ISI Web of Knowledge etc.
Ulrich International periodical directory.
Journals and Newspapers.
Recognition of International Universities by HEC through Authentic Resources (International Handbook of Universities by UNESCO).
Provision of books.
