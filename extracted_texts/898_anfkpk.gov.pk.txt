Anti Narcotics Force (ANF) at Torkham boarder terminal seized an unattended suspicious baggage
Anti Narcotics Force (ANF) at Torkham boarder terminal seized a unattended suspicious baggage and recovered 0.750 KGs of Heroine. Narcotics was concealed in plastic shopping bag, lying near zero-point Torkham boarder.
Case has been registered, and further investigation in progress.
