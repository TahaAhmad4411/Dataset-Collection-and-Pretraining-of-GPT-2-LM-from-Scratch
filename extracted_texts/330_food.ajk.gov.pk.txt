azad jamu and kashmir food department
The AJ&K Food Department was initially established in 1949, their first head quarter was at Muree. The Department was named as Civil Supply and Transport of AJ&K and Northern Areas
About Food Department
The Azad Jammu and Kashmir (AJK) Food Department plays a vital role in ensuring food security and supply chain management within the region. It oversees the distribution of essential food commodities, regulates food prices, and implements policies to support the agricultural sector. Through its efforts, the AJK Food Department contributes to the well-being and nutrition of the population in this picturesque region of Pakistan.

Learn About Our vision and misson
our mission
Our mission is to ensure the timely availability of high-quality wheat flour for the residents of AJ&K. We are committed to offering this essential staple at an affordable price to meet the needs of our community. Our goal is to consistently deliver top-notch wheat flour, prioritizing both quality and affordability for the people we serve in AJ&K.
our vision
To ensure modern and efficient food distribution system which can contribute towards food security and nutrition in term of availability, access, utilization and stability.

History Of Food Department
The Food Department AJ&K/GB was commonly known as “CIVIL SUPPLIES & TRANSPORT
Department” till 1975 which was entrusted along responsibilities of supplying and distribution of
commodities to the people under the administration of M/O Kashmir Affairs and Northern
Affairs Division GoP.  In 1976 two separate Departments of Food has been created for AJ&K & Gilgit Baltistan
(Northern Areas). Directorate Of Food AJ&AmpK Was Established In Rawalpindi.
Functions of Food Department
Procurement food items as wheat and sugar etc
Distribution of Wheat and Atta and essential commodities specified by government for  this purpose
Control over prices and supervision of Ration Depots and markets
Sanction and control of Flour Mills
Purchase of stores and capital good for the Department
Services matters except those entrusted to Services and General Administration  Department
