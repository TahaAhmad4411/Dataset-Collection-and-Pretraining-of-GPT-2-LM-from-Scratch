ANF Peshawar arrested a Nigerian National and recovered 1.187 KGs of Cocaine
Anti Narcotics Force (ANF) Peshawar conducted an operation at Bacha Khan International Airport  (BKIAP) and arrested a Nigerian National at arrival Hall BKIAP and recovered 1.187 KGs of Cocaine from his personal possession which was concealed in 76 capsules.
The value of seized Narcotics is estimated to be around 10 Million Rupees (PKR). The accused was traveling on Flight No QR-600
Case has been registered, and further investigation in progress.
