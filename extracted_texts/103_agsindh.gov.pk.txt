Office of the A.G.Sindh celebrates 77 years of Independence Day
The Office of the Accountant General Sindh celebrated the 77th Independence Day of Pakistan with enthusiasm and patriotism. The celebration likely included flag hoisting, national anthem singing, and various cultural and patriotic activities to honor the country’s journey since its independence in 1947. Such events often serve to remind government employees and the general public of the importance of their roles in nation-building and the responsibilities they bear as citizens.
