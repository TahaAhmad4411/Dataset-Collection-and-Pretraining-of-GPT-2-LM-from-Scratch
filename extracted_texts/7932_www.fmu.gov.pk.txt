MOU Between China and Pakistan
The signing ceremony of Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the Financial Monitoring Unit of Pakistan and the Analysis Center of Pepole’s Bank of China of the People’s  Republic of China on cooperation in the exchange of financial intelligence related to money laundering and terrorism financing was held in Canberra, Australia on August 18, 2019. The MOU was signed by Mr. Mansoor Hassan Siddiqui, Director General, FMU Pakistan and Mr. BAO Mingyou, deputy director general of AML Bureau the People’s Bank of China. The Governor, State Bank of Pakistan, Dr. Reza Baqir was also present at the signing ceremony
