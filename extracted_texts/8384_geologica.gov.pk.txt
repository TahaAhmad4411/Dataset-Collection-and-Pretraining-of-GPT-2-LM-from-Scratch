Prof. Dr. Sajjad Ahmad
Dr. Sajjad Ahmad is currently working as Director General Geological Survey of Pakistan. He has vast expertise in the field of Earth Sciences including Structural Geology, Remote Sensing, Seismic Interpretation and Mineral Exploration. He served the Department of Geology, University of Peshawar as Professor and Chairman for a period of 27 years.
Dr. Sajjad Ahmad has a number of national and international publications to his credit. He has convened and participated in several seminars, workshops and conferences both in Pakistan and abroad.
In addition, Dr. Sajjad Ahmad has also provided consulting services to the Oil and Gas sector of Pakistan in the field of Geological Mapping, Structural Modelling and Prospect Identification, covering most of the parts of the fold and thrust belts of Pakistani Himalaya.

Prof.Dr. Qasim Jan
Dr. M. Qasim Jan is an Adviser in OIC Standing Committee on Scientific & Technological Cooperation (COMSTECH), Islamabad and Professor Emeritus, Center of Excellence in Geology, University of Peshawar. Previously, he held a number of eminent positions in various education institutions like the Vice Chancellor, Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad, Sarhad University, Peshawar and University of Peshawar. He received his Master’s degree from the University of Oregon, USA and obtained his Ph.D degree from the University of London, UK.
Dr. Qasim has remarkable research contributions in,
A better understanding of the geodynamic evolution of northwest Himalaya-Kohistan-Karakoram-Hindukush
Petrologic and tectonic evolution of the lower and middle continental crust in general and Kohistan magmatic arc in particular, with special emphasis on the Kamila amphibolites, and Chilas, Jijal and Sapat mafic-ultramafic complexes
Chemical mineralogy of chromite occurrences of Pakistan
Tectonic configuration of gemstones of northern Pakistan
Recent investigation related to neotectonics and earthquakes in northern Pakistan and Balochistan
Petrology of the Creto-Tertiary Raskoh and Chagai magmatic arcs
Realization that the Proterozoic, A-type granitoids of Nagar Parkar are emplaced in a calc-alkaline island arc foundation and were followed by bimodal mafic-silicic magmatism,
He is an author of 278 papers and short communications published locally and internationally. He is the joint author of the seminal book “Geology and Tectonics of Pakistan” (with AH Kazmi, 1997), which won the Best Book award in Natural Sciences(1995–97 period) from National Book Foundation of Pakistan.

Prof. Dr Hafiz Ur Rehman
Dr. Hafiz U. Rehman currently works as Associate Professor at the Department of Earth Sciences, Graduate School of Science and Engineering, Kagoshima University, Japan.
Hafiz does research in Mineralogy, Petrology, Geochemistry, and Geochronology. Main research interests are in High Pressure metamorphic rocks, particularly in the Himalayan region. Hafiz is a regular member of several national and international scientific societies related to Earth Sciences (including MSA, JPGU, JGS, JAMS) and executive committee member of the International Eclogite Conference.
He published more than 50 scientific articles including review papers) in International Impact Factor journals, and presented research results (including invited talks) in numerous National and International conferences. He has also served as Guest editor of Lithos, Journal of Asian Earth Sciences, and The Island Arc.
He is an active reviewer for various international journals (Geology, Gondwana Research, Journal of Metamorphic Geology, Journal of Asian Earth Sciences, Lithos, Island Arc, Journal of Mineralogical and Petrological Sciences, Minerals, Mineralogy Petrology, and so on).

Prof. Dr. Rehanul Haq Siddiqui
Dr. Rehanul Haq Siddiqui is currently working as Chairman, Board of Directors, Sandak Metals Ltd., under the Ministry of Energy (Petroleum Division) Government of Pakistan. Previously worked as Director Geological Survey of Pakistan and served as Professor in the Balochistan University of Information Technology, Engineering and Management Sciences, Quetta. He has also worked as visiting Professor in the China University of Geosciences at Wuhan. Currently he is affiliated as visiting Professor with the University of Balochistan at Quetta.

Published several research papers in various National and International Journals and presented many scientific papers in various national and international seminars, conferences and symposia. Organized and conducted many in house and National level training workshops at GARL, Islamabad and University of Balochistan, Quetta.

Prof. Dr. Tahseen Ullah Khan
Dr. Tahseenullah Khan did his PhD in 1994 from NCE in Geology, University of Peshawar. He is Senior Professor in the Department of Earth and Environment Sciences, Bahria University, Islamabad. He has been Professor in Naruto University of Education; Honorary Professor in China Geological Survey, Xian Centre, China; Assistant Project Manager, Gemstone Corporation of Pakistan and Voluntary retired Deputy Director, Geological Survey of Pakistan.
Dr. Khan remained as Post-graduate program coordinator and Head of Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences, Bahria University, Islamabad campus. Due to his excellent research record, Government of Japan entrusted upon him Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) fellowship award. He is the member of Board of Governors of National Centre of Excellence in Geology, University of Peshawar; Member of the Northwestern Geology Editorial Committee, China Geological Survey, Xi’an Centre China; Associate Editor of International Journal of Economic and Environmental Geology; Former Editor of Geologica, a research bulletin of Geoscience Advance Research Laboratory, Islamabad; Former member of the Resource Geology Journal of Japan; Member of the Expert Panel of Pakistan Science Foundation for evaluation of national and international funded projects; Ex-focal person on the 2005 Kashmir Earthquake on behalf of Geological Survey of Pakistan and Senior Vice President and Joint secretary of National Geological Society of Pakistan.
As a Senior Vice President of National Geological Society of Pakistan, Dr. Khan organized many seminars, conferences, workshops and trainings including the 4th and 5th Geological Congress of Pakistan and First International Workshop on Geoinformatics and workshop on Geological Materials and Aggregates of Pakistan. He worked as a Team Member of the Technical Committee for the International Conference on 8th October 2005 Earthquake, organized by Geological Survey of Pakistan at Islamabad edited the extended abstract volume for the conference.
Dr. Khan has published more than 160 research papers in national and international journals and extended abstracts, proceedings, in-house journals and international reports with total impact factor 67.391 and 710 citations. He published a book “Gems and Gemmology in Pakistan in 2010” as a lead author, Special Publication of the Geological Survey of Pakistan. He has participated in 14 international conferences/workshops in Europe, Asia and North Africa.
Dr. Khan has supervised 40 post-graduate students, co-supervised two PhD scholars and currently supervising six PhD scholars including one foreigner.

Dr. Shahid Iqbal
Dr. Shahid Iqbal is presently working as assistant professor in the Department of Earth Sciences, Quaid-iAzam University (QAU), Islamabad, Pakistan.
He did his PhD and Post-Doc from the Department of Geology, University of Vienna, Austria. He did his BS and M.Sc. in Geology from the Department of Geology, University of Peshawar, Pakistan, and M.Phil. (Geology) from the Department of Earth Sciences QAU. He has worked as visiting guest lecturer at the Department of Geology, University of Vienna. He is interested in the studying Paleo-climates and Paleo-environments and has worked on the sedimentology and paleo-climatic fluctuations during the Permian to Jurassic time on the NW passive Tethyan margin of the Indian Plate.
He is specialized in using framework composition analysis, sediments geochemistry, bulk mineralogical composition using XRD, clay mineralogy, heavy minerals analysis. Additionally, he works on seismic reflection data and its use in basin modelling and tectonic reconstructions. Currently, he is working on the Triassic-Jurassic transition focusing mainly on the paleo-climatic fluctuations and sedimentation style. He is also working on the sedimentation and paleo-climatic fluctuations during the Pleistocene-Holocene glaciation-de-glaciation and the emerging subject of the Anthropocene.

DR. ASGHAR ALI
I am an Associate Professor and HEC-recognized PhD supervisor in the Department of Geology at the University of Peshawar. Additionally, I hold the position of Director of the Centralized Resource Laboratory at the University of Peshawar.
My research interests include ore geochemistry, structural geology (micro- to macro-scale structures associated with cyclic orogenic events), petrology, geochronology, thermodynamics, and regional/global tectonics. I am focused on establishing strong connections between structural geology, regional/global tectonics, metamorphic geology, and thermodynamics.
I am particularly interested in utilizing structures to comprehend the complex history of multiply deformed rocks.

Prof. Dr. Anna Trave
PhD in 1992 on Sedimentology of carbonate rocks. Post-doctoral research in Montpellier University (France) working on fluid flow and faults.Teaching at Barcelona University from 2000. My current research interest is in fluid flow during compressive and extensive deformation. For compressive settings, I am mostly dealing with the Pyrenean fold and thrust belt but other orogens are also studied. For the extensive settings I am mostly dealing with the Neogene Penedès and Vallès haf-graben in the Northeastern part of the Iberian Peninsula. I also work on fracture-related dolomitization together with other diagenetic processes affecting carbonate rocks.

Dr. Alessandro Iannace
He is professor of Sedimentary Geology at Federico II University in Napoli, Italy. After a Master at Paris VI University on Plio-Pleistocene isotope stratigraphy, he got his  PhD at Napoli University. His main research focus has been the sedimentology and diagenesis of shallow water carbonates, including their relationships with base metal mineralization and reservoir characterization.
The main goals of these researches have been the development of a paleoenvironmental model for the microbial biocontructions of Upper Triassic of Italy, the distinction of many dolomitization models in the Mesozoic carbonates of southern Italy, the characterization of epigenetic hydrothermal dolomitization in metamorhpsed carbonates od SW Sardinia.
More recently, in the frame of industry collaboration, developed models of mechanical stratigraphy as a tool for HC production in Southern Italy and revised the stratigraphy and structural geology of Lurestan, Iran, and western Greece.
