PM AJ&K, Chaudhry Anwar ul Haq, handed over the keys of new vehicles to Deputy Inspector General of Police Reserves.
Date
30.10.24
The Azad Kashmir government has provided new vehicles to various police stations across the region. The Prime Minister of Azad Jammu and Kashmir, Chaudhry Anwar ul Haq, handed over the keys of these new vehicles to Deputy Inspector General of Police Reserve Dr. Liaqat. Senior Minister and Interior Minister Col. (Retd) Waqar Ahmad Noor, Deputy Speaker Chaudhry Riaz Ahmad, Information Minister Pir Muhammad Mazhar Saeed Shah, TEVTA Minister Sardar Amir Altaf, Food Minister Chaudhry Akbar Ibrahim, Forestry Minister Akmal Hussain Sargala, and Inspector General of Police Rana Abdul Jabbar, along with other relevant officials, were present on the occasion. The Inspector General of Police briefed the Prime Minister, who then inspected the vehicles.
The Azad Kashmir government has provided 19 new 4×4 single-cabin vehicles for various police stations. Each district received two vehicles, while Haveli Kahuta received one. These vehicles were allocated to different stations based on need. The addition of these vehicles is expected to enhance police efficiency. The new vehicles provided by the Azad Kashmir government will be used exclusively for police station operations and will not be utilized for any squad or protocol purposes.
