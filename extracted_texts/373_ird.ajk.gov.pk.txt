For Tax Year 2024, Income Tax Return Filing Date Extended till 30 October, 2024.
3rd October, 2024
Central Board of Revenue is pleased to communicate that the date of filing of Income Tax retum for the Tax Year (TY) 2024, for the persons who are required to file their retums by September 30th,2024 is hereby extended upto October 30, 2024 in view of the requests from various trade bodies and general public.
