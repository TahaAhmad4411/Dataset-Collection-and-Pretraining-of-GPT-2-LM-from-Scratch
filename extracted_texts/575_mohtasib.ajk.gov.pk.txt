Tour of Mohtasib AJK to Federal Tax Ombudsman Pakistan (April 14,2023).
(April 14, 2023) Mohtasib AJK Chaudhary Muhammad Naseem visited Federal Tax Ombudsman on the invitation of Doctor Asif Mehmood Jah (Federal Tax Ombudsman Pakistan). Mohtasib AJK also visited the office of Tax Ombudsman and joined the “Aftaar Dinner” organized in the honor of Mohtasib AJK.
