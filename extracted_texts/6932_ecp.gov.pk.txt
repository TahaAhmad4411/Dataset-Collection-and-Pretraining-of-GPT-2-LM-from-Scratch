Mr. Shamshad Khan
Director General (Training)
Date of Birth     :          01-01-1973
Nationality        :          Pakistani
Religion            :          Islam
Domicile           :          South Waziristan
Education
Did Matric from Zam Public School Tank, F.Sc from University Wensam College Dera Ishmail Khan, Bachelors of Arts from Islamia College Peshawar and Masters in History from Quaid-E-Azam University Islamabad.
Job Performed
Joined Election Commission of Pakistan in 1997 as Assistant Election Commissioner and remained posted in different capacities at district level, Regional, Provincial Head Quarters and ECP Secretariat Islamabad. Presently posted as Director General (Elections) in ECP Secretariat Islamabad. Performed duties as District Returning Officer, Returning Officer, Registration Officer and Delimitation Authority in Bye-Election and Local Government Delimitation.
Trainings
Attended MCMC (2013), SMC (2016) and NMC (2020) courses.Attended Electoral and Legislative administration course under JICA in Tokyo Japan.Attended different Training Courses and is a bridge accredited facilitator.
