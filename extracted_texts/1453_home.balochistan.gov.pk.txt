Departments and Divisions
The
Home and Tribal Affairs Department
operates through several key divisions and wings that help it achieve its objectives:
Law Enforcement Wing
Oversees the operations and coordination of police, Levies, and other security forces in the province.
Implements crime control measures and ensures the safety of citizens.
Tribal Affairs Wing
Focuses on resolving disputes among tribal communities.
Works to integrate tribal communities into the provincial socio-economic framework.
Public Safety Wing
Develops strategies for crime prevention and safety education.
Promotes the adoption of safety regulations across the province.
Disaster Management Wing
Coordinates disaster preparedness and response strategies with the PDMA.
Provides support during natural disasters, including flood relief and earthquake response.
