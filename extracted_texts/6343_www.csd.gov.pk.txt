Free Home Delivery Terms & Conditions
1. Free home delivery service will be offered on shopping of minimum Rs. 2,000/-
2. The delivery time will be 02 hrs (according to location) *delivery can be delayed in peak rush hrs.
3. Free Delivery service is available within 10 km radius of CSD shop. Orders placed beyond 10 km radius will get charged (Please confirm the charges prior to your order).
4. Please call us on 051-111-273-111 to confirm if your delivery location is covered.
5. Payment will be made in Cash on the arrival of our rider.
6. Orders need to be made before shop closing hours. (Confirm from our customer services representative while placing an order).
7. Orders placed after shop closing hours, will be delivered next day.
