The principal objectives of Chaklala food control authority are:
Protecting public health by reducing the risk of foodborne illness
Protecting consumers from unsanitary, unwholesome, mislabelled or adulterated food
Contributing to economic development by maintaining consumer confidence in the food system and providing a sound regulatory foundation for domestic trade in food
