A meeting was held under the chairmanship of the Prime Minister of the Azad Government of the State of Jammu and Kashmir, Chaudhry Anwarul Haq
Date
18.09.24
A meeting was held under the chairmanship of the Prime Minister of the Azad Government of the State of Jammu and Kashmir, Chaudhry Anwarul Haq, regarding matters related to Azad Jammu and Kashmir University. The meeting was attended by Minister for Higher Education Malik Zafar Iqbal, Advisor to the Honorable President Sardar Imtiaz Ahmad, Chief Secretary Daud Muhammad Bareech, Secretary to the Honorable President Muhammad Idrees Abbasi, Secretary Higher Education Chaudhry Muhammad Tayyab, Vice Chancellor of the University Dr. Muhammad Kaleem Abbasi, and Secretary Finance Islam Zaib Khan, who joined via video link.
Prime Minister Azad Kashmir, Chaudhry Anwarul Haq, stated that all possible steps will be taken to improve the university in Azad Kashmir. He emphasized that the government will prioritize resolving the financial matters of the university. The Prime Minister further said that institutions must be strengthened so they can play a exemplary role.
