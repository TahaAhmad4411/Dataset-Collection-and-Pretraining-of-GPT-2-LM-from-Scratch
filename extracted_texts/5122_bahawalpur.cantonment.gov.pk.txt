WHY CANTT PUBLIC SCHOOL
We at Cantt Public School aim to provide a friendly, secure and happy environment for our students, where friendly, qualified and experienced staff, care for children in a peaceful atmosphere to develop each child’s potential and self-confidence. We shall encourage the children’s interests and allow them to explore their intellectual curiosity, develop their language skills, critical thinking, use their imagination, enhance their creativity and enjoy physical play. Cantt Public School will help them to develop positive social skills, build relationships, and help them gain awareness of and understand about the world around them.
We believe that all children should have equal access to all areas of the curriculum, and not be disadvantaged because of gender, learning difficulties, family background, home language or lifestyle.
We Give a Dream to Every Child.
State of the Art Facilities
We at Cantt Public School offering STEAM program which is one of the unique features of our campus, whereby our students will be actively engaged in learning about Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts & Maths at the same time through structured hands on activities.
