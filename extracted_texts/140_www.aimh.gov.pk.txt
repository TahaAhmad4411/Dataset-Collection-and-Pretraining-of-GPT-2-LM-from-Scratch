5th Internship Batch
Eligibility Criteria
Regular students of HEC recognized universities in the discipline of social sciences (undergraduate BS 6
th
Semester onward only and postgraduate) with Min 3 CGPA. Soft copy/ image of the last semester’s result to be attached with mail or sent by WhatsApp.
Pakistani nationals only.
How to Apply
Mail your particulars (as per the specified form below) to
Internship@aimh.gov.pk
.
Or WhatsApp particulars (
Bio Data Form Below
) at 0302-9700658.
Submission of particulars latest by
9 June 2024
.
Note
Only shortlisted candidates will be called for interviews.
No TA/ DA is admissible for candidates called for an interview.
A modest honorarium will be paid to interns, upon completion of the internship programme.
No transportation and accommodation is offered for selected candidates during the internship.
