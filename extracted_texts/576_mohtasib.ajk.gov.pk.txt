Ombudsman’s Message
I am pleased to extend a warm welcome to you on behalf of the Office of the Ombudsman AJK, and introduce our website. Our website is specifically designed to provide direct access to all individuals, especially citizens who have been affected by the actions of any government functionary in AJK. Our aim is to offer information and guidance on how the office of the Ombudsman AJK can assist you. The website is user-friendly and interactive to ensure easy navigation. We have introduced information technology to promote a paperless environment, aligning with our goal to be highly enabled.
