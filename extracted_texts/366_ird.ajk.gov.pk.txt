Prime Minister AJ&K
(Chaudhry Anwar Ul Haq)
The Inland Revenue Department under the supervision of AJK CBR is playing an important role towards strengthening the State economy by exploring all revenue resources. State people should perform their active role and come forward to further strengthen the State economy by payment of maximum taxes in cooperation with the Department.
PM, AJ&K
Chief Secretary AJ&K
(Mr. Khushal Khan)
CS, AJ&K
Chairman AJK CBR &
Secretary IRD
(Mr. Muhammad Raqeeb Khan)
In every society, realization of taxes significantly formulates the economy in various manners. With the similar objective, the Inland Revenue Secretariat is concentrating on ways and means to deploy funds accumulated from taxes for public interests in precise method as the Government safeguards the public funds.
Chairman
Taxes are a primary source of revenue for governments. This revenue is essential for education, healthcare, transportation, and public safety. Without adequate tax revenues, governments would struggle to maintain these essential services and infrastructure.
The same helps in stabilizing the economy by influencing economic activity. For example, progressive taxation helps to reduce income inequality whereas taxes and subsidies contribute to overall economic stability and growth.
Imposition of taxes is used to redistribute wealth and addressing income inequality. Higher income individuals pay a higher percentage of their income in taxes that help to reduce disparities between the wealthy and the less affluent, promoting social equity.
Taxation is fundamental to the development of nations as it provides the financial resources necessary for public services, promotes economic stability and growth, supports infrastructure development, and helps in the redistribution of wealth. Effective tax policies and administration are crucial for ensuring that taxation contributes positively to a nation’s development.
Secretary AJK CBR
(Sardar Zafar Mahmud Khan)
