CHAIRMAN CDA MESSAGE
CH. MUHAMMAD ALI RANDHAWA
(CHAIRMAN CDA)
Our mission is to transform Islamabad into a city of excellence, where innovation meets heritage and where progress seamlessly blends with nature.
We are dedicated to creating spaces that inspire, services that exceed expectations, and an environment where every resident can thrive. Let us work together to ensure cleaner streets, greener spaces, and a healthier environment for all. Let's plant more trees, dispose of waste responsibly, and be mindful of our ecological footprint. Your voice matters, and I urge you to be an active participant in the evolution of our capital.
Together, let's embark on this remarkable journey of building a city that not only shines today but continues to illuminate for generations to come.
Together, let's shape a dynamic and sustainable future for
                                Islamabad,
                                where every resident's well-being is our priority.
