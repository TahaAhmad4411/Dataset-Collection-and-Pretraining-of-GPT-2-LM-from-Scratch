CSD Rebate Card (for Serving Armed Forces)
1
Eligibility
Armed forces personnel (Army, Navy, Air force) are entitled for issuance of CSD Rebate Cards.
2
Procedure for Issuance of Card
1. Customer will buy the application form from any CSD shop worth Rs. 200.
2.  For central preparation of Rebate Cards, all units / fmns will forward essentially required data of the authorized persons to Head Office CSD through Application Form which is available at all CSD outlets.
3. The formation will send filled application form to CSD Head Office (IT Div) in a sealed envelope under Per/Conf covering letter at the address below:
Card Section, IT Division,
CSD Head Office
265 – Muhammad Hussain Road
PO Box 1039, Rawalpindi Cantt.
4. All cards after printing will be dispatched to unit/fmns concerned for handing over to the applicant.
3
Activation
1. Card must be activated before 1st use.
2. Customer will provide CNIC to CSD rep for activation.
4
Discount
10% discount on shopping of grocery / retail (from CSD Retail Shops)
3% discount on shopping of electronics / home appliances (from HPBCs)
CSD Rebate Card (for Retired Armed Forces)
1
Eligibility
Retired Armed forces personnel (Army, Navy, Air force) are entitled for issuance of CSD Rebate Cards
2
Procedure for Issuance of Card
1. Customer will buy the application form from any CSD shop worth Rs. 200.
2. After filling the necessary information, Application Form will be submitted to Manager CSD shop.
3. Manager will forward the Application Form to Head Office (IT Div, Card Section) for preparation of card.
4. After necessary scrutiny of documents, IT Div will print the Card and dispatch to customer on his / her mailing address mentioned on form.
3
Activation
1. Card must be activated before 1st use.
2. Customer will provide CNIC to CSD rep for activation.
4
Discount
5% discount on shopping of grocery / retail (from CSD Retail Shops)
