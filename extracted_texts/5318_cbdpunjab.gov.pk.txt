INTRODUCTION
The Punjab Central Business District Development Authority (PCBDDA), also known as Central Business District Punjab (CBD Punjab), transforms Lahore, Pakistan’s urban canvas. Situated near the city’s economic hub, this initiative covers extensive land, aiming to establish Lahore as a distinguished global business hub. With a preliminary financial estimate ranging from PKR 2,700 billion to 3,000 billion, the project promises to redefine urban development paradigms in the heart of Pakistan.
ABOUT US
Punjab Central Business District Development Authority (PCBDDA) also known as Central Business District Punjab (CBD Punjab) is an authorized body incorporated to establish and promote environment friendly urban regeneration projects on vertical principles. A pre-financial feasibility has been determined for around PKR 2700B-3000B based on development as economic advancement and shall generate employment in various sectors. Development shall also enhance vertical activity in surroundings especially within the surrounding Gulberg area with a boost in major business activity. The Development has been categorized in five districts namely, Commercial Zone, Digital District, Residential District, Entertainment and Government Zone, each district having unique characteristics & dynamics, connected through urban green infrastructure developed by renowned international architects. Other unique features of the development include a History Museum and Jinnah Monument illustrating the rich history of the Walton Airport. Botanical Gardens and Green spaces are part of the project to complement the green architecture of this Unique yet modern development. This is a project to put Pakistan on the map of Global Business Districts.

SHAPING THE FUTURE OF PUNJAB WITH CBD PUNJAB
In the heart of Lahore, Pakistan, the visionary Punjab Central Business District Development Authority (PCBDDA), or Central Business District Punjab (CBD Punjab), is turning urban dreams into reality. As Pakistan's inaugural Central Business District, CBD Punjab is at the forefront of redefining the nation's urban landscape. Functioning as an authorized body, CBD Punjab focuses on environmentally friendly urban regeneration projects with a commitment to comprehensive, sustainable, and vibrant vertical development principles.
THE VISION UNVEILED

FACILITIES
LEED CERTIFIED URBAN VISION
Committed to energy-efficient green infrastructure designs, aiming for LEED certification, promoting a pleasant living environment in well-maintained streets and public spaces.
WATER TREATMENT PLANT
Featuring a state-of-the-art facility purifying water from various sources, ensuring clean and safe water for residents and businesses.
RAINWATER HARVESTING
Committed to sustainability, our water treatment plant conserves rainwater, an eco-friendly practice protecting the environment.
CBD PUNJAB’S GRID STATION
Ensuring uninterrupted power supply through concealed electrical infrastructure and a dedicated grid station guarantees seamless power flow for businesses and homes.
UNINTERRUPTED UNDERGROUND ELECTRICITY
To ensure a steady electricity supply, we’ve invested in an underground electrical infrastructure, maintaining a dependable and safe power supply for businesses and homes.
SMART SECURITY
Employing cutting-edge technology for a safe and secure environment, including advanced surveillance systems, access control, facial recognition, and intelligent monitoring.
SMART CITIES COMPONENTS
Embracing smart city technology, including advanced telecommunications and intelligent energy management, makes the city more efficient, secure, and modern.
WASTE MANAGEMENT AND RECYCLING
A comprehensive waste management system collects, sorts, and recycles litter, reducing waste to landfills and minimizing environmental impact.
UNDERGROUND PARKING
The project includes underground parking facilities, ensuring efficient vehicle parking, maintaining a neat city surface, and offering ample space for residents and workers.
INTRA-CITY CONNECTIVITY
Strategically connected to key areas, ensuring easy access to Main Boulevard Gulberg, Walton Road, Ferozepur Road, and proposed links, making commuting convenient.
EMERGENCY SERVICES
Safety is paramount at CBD Punjab, with state-of-the-art fire, health, and rescue services ensuring a rapid and effective response.
SOLAR PV
Utilizing solar photovoltaic (PV) technology with installed solar panels harnessing sun energy, promoting sustainability and reducing environmental impact.
