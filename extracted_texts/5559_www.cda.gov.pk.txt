Public Grievance and Complaints Cell has been established for both Capital Development Authority,
                Islamabad and Metropolitan Corporation Islamabad. The Cell is headed by Chief Complaint Officer.

                working days (Monday - Friday).
Phone: 051-9253016 and 051-9252962
If you have any queries, let us know and we will get back to you as soon as possible.
