ENGINEERING WING
Directorates Under The Wing
Engineering Wing
Engineering wing is involved in all the electrical and mechanical works of the city. It also undertakes
                all new construction works which are either self-financed by CDA or funded by any other government
                department/office. The wing is responsible for the production, treatment, conduction and distribution of
                potable water from different surfaces as well as groundwater resources to the end-user. The wing also
                looks after the maintenance of roads, footpaths, drainage systems and landscaping of commercial areas of
                the city.
Bulk Water Management Directorate
Responsible for O&M of Simly Dam and Metropolitan Water Supply Project (Khanpur-I)
The Bulk Water Management directorate within the Capital Development Authority (CDA) carries
                            the crucial responsibility of overseeing the operation and maintenance of Simly Dam and the
                            Metropolitan Water Supply Project Khanpur-I. This directorate is structured into two
                            integral divisions namely, the Simly Dam Division, in collaboration with the office of the
                            resident engineer from WAPDA, and the Khanpur Dam Division. The water sourced from Simly Dam
                            serves as the exclusive supply for Islamabad, while Khanpur Dam's water undergoes treatment
                            at the Sangjani Water Treatment Plant before being distributed to the twin cities of
                            Islamabad and Rawalpindi in accordance with the approved allocation.

Water Supply Directorate
Responsible for; production, transmission and distribution of water from ground and surface
                            sources
The Water Supply Directorate plays a crucial role in ensuring the availability of clean and
                            potable water to the residents of Islamabad. Here's an overview of its responsibilities and
                            operations:
Water Production and Treatment: The directorate is responsible for producing, treating,
                                and ensuring the quality of water from various sources, both surface and ground. This
                                includes the operation of Simly and Sangjani water treatment plants, Shahdara, Saidpur,
                                Korang, and Noorpur Water Works, the Poona Faquiran Augmentation Scheme, and 189 tube
                                Wells.
Distribution Network: The water is distributed across the city through an extensive
                                system of conduction, service reservoirs, sumps/intermediate pumping stations, and a
                                distribution network. This network ensures that water is delivered efficiently to
                                consumers.
Small Filtration Plants: In addition to the major water treatment facilities, the
                                directorate has installed 37 small water filtration plants in various sectors of
                                Islamabad, providing clean drinking water to residents in specific areas.
Water Tanker Service: To address water shortage complaints and ensure an uninterrupted
                                water supply, the directorate operates a water tanker service comprising 36 tankers,
                                which can be requested by consumers when needed, typically for a nominal charge.
The water supply directorate operates through four divisions:
Production Division-I
Production Division-II
Distribution Division (North)
Distribution Division (South)
The production and treatment processes take place at filtration plants and tube wells, after
                            which water is transported to service reservoirs and intermediate pumping stations. From
                            there, it's distributed through the city's network to reach individual households and
                            consumers. This comprehensive approach to water supply management ensures that residents
                            have access to clean and reliable water for their daily needs.
