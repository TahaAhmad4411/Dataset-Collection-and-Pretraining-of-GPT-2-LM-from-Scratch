Our Subsidiaries
Staff welfare is important for not only the employees themselves but for the entire organization. Promoting staff welfare initiatives is good for the prevention of stress and for the creation of a positive work environment where people and organizational performance can thrive.
ASF Foundation
Established on 24 April 2014 to provide and generate funds for welfare measures and benefits of Shuhada Families, serving and ex-servicemen of ASF and their families through various welfare schemes.
View Website
ASF Medical Centers
Provide and arrange good health care to Shuhada Families, serving and ex-servicemen of ASF and their families at major airports.
ASF Schools and Colleges
Provide and facilitate quality education to the children of Shuhada, serving and ex-servicemen of ASF at major airports.

ASF Security Private Limited
ASF Security (Pvt) Ltd, is rapidly growing security providers in Pakistan with a team of highly trained and capable professionals. Established since 2015, ASF Security (Pvt) Ltd is incorporated under section 32 of the ‘Companies Ordinance 1984’ with the SECP, as well as fully licensed and compliant with all federal, provincial and local regulatory standards / agencies.

Firm and Courteous
ASF, an executive department of Ministry of Aviation was established in 1976 under the Airports Security Force Act LXXVII of 1975. 
                            ASF has been mandated with the task of providing security to all Civil Aviation Airports of Pakistan. Currently ASF is securing 25 operational 
                            Airports (12 International and 13 Domestic Airports). ASF ensures security of Aircrafts, Passengers, their luggage, mail along with proper 
                            conduct of persons and vehicles within the limits of airports and aerodromes.
