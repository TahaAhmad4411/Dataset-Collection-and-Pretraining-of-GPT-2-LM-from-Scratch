Message By District & Sessions Judge
Justice, a supreme virtue, is to be applied to every rich and poor, young and old, ally and enemy, Muslim and Non-muslim and so forth without fear, favour and all kinds of discriminations. ALLAH SWT says in the Holy Quran, “Surely, Allah commands you to deliver trusts to those entitled to them, and that, when you judge between people, judge with justice. Surely, excellent is the exhortation Allah gives you. Surely, ALLAH is All-Hearing, All-Seeing” (4:58) District Judiciary Mansehra is determined and striving its best to administer justice in its true spirit and in accordance with the law.
