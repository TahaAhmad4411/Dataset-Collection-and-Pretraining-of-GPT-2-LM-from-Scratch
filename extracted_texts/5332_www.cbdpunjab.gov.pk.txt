CBD PUNJAB – KSA SIGN MOUs FOR BILATERAL PROJECTS
A high-level business delegation, led by the then Prime Minister of Pakistan, visited Riyadh in October 2021. Later in July 2022, prominent Saudi businessmen and entrepreneurs were invited by Punjab Board of Investment and Trade (PBIT). The delegation was led by Chairman Saudi-Pak Business Council, H.E. Mr. Fahad Al Bash. A team of CBD Punjab accompanied the delegation in their visits to Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad. The fruitful sessions brought success to PCBDDA as CBD Punjab signed a contract for bilateral cooperation with a notable Saudi construction group for infrastructural project for public development at the Punjab Central Business District Development Authority.
