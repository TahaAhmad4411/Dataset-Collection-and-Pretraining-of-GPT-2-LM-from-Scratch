CBD PUNJAB CELEBRATES PAKISTAN DAY WITH PATRIOTIC FERVOR, REAFFIRMS COMMITMENT TO NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT
To commemorate Pakistan Day, Punjab Central Business District Development Authority (PCBDDA) conducted a flag hoisting ceremony at CBD House Punjab.
To commemorate Pakistan Day, Punjab Central Business District Development Authority (PCBDDA) conducted a flag hoisting ceremony at CBD House Punjab. The event paid tribute to the historic resolution passed by the All-India Muslim League that led to the establishment of Pakistan. Attendees included COO Brigadier (R) Mansoor Janjua, Directorate heads, and CBD Punjab staff members. The ceremony featured the singing of the national anthem, evoking a sense of national pride and honor.
Addressing the gathering, COO Brigadier (R) Mansoor Janjua emphasized the significance of the day, stating, "Today marks a pivotal moment in our nation's history. As an authority, CBD Punjab plays a vital role in the development of Punjab and Pakistan. We remain committed to supporting our country's growth by providing innovative business solutions and contributing to overall national development."
The event exemplified CBD Punjab's passion and dedication to the progress of the nation. It underscored the historical significance of Pakistan Day and its deep-rooted connection to the country's heritage. CBD Punjab has played a key role in promoting modern business practices and infrastructure development in Punjab, establishing Pakistan's first business district based on vertical principles. This groundbreaking initiative has significantly contributed to the economic growth of the region.
