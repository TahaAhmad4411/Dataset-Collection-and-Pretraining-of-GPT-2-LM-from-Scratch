Director General/CEO’s Message
The Hydrocarbon Development Institute of Pakistan (HDIP) serves as the nation’s premier Petroleum Research and Development (R&D) organization. Established in 1971 as a body corporate under the Ministry of Energy (Petroleum Division), and re-established under Act No. 1 of 2006, HDIP has steadfastly pursued its mission to drive scientific and technological advancements in the petroleum sector, encompassing both upstream and downstream activities.
Over the years, HDIP has become a pioneering institution, playing a critical role in the exploration, development, and sustainable utilization of Pakistan’s oil and gas resources. HDIP has state-of-the-art ISO-9001 certified and ISO-17025 Accredited laboratories with trained professionals who adhere to the highest international standards.
We believe that the future of energy lies in collaboration. That’s why we work closely with industry experts, academic institutions, and policymakers to ensure that our work not only advances scientific knowledge but also has a tangible, positive impact on the industry.
Since 1992, HDIP has maintained the National Energy Database, a vital resource for Pakistan’s energy sector. We are also proud to publish the Pakistan Energy Yearbook and the Pakistan Journal of Hydrocarbon Research, providing essential technical information to industry stakeholders.
Our success is deeply rooted in the exceptional quality and dedication of our team. I am proud to lead an organization of individuals who are genuinely passionate about progress. Their energy and commitment will be key to HDIP’s continued role in shaping a prosperous future for the sector.
A key element of our success as a research institute is also creating an environment where HDIP is recognized as an outstanding place to work. We are committed to attracting, engaging, and retaining top talent, ensuring that our people thrive and contribute to our collective vision.
HDIP remains committed to supporting Pakistan’s energy sector through continuous innovation, rigorous research, and an unwavering dedication to safety and quality standards.
