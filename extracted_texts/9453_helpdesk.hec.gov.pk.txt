How to Lodge a Complain
Posted by Amna Javed on 26 October 2017 01:12 PM
Please follow the below given steps to lodge a complain against your particular univeristy:
Log in to your helpdesk account
Go to "Submit a Ticket"
Select the option "Univeristy"
Select the province of your main campus of univeristy
Select the univeristy name from the drop down menu
Click on "NEXT"
Select your registartion number, campus and department
Select the category to "Grievance"
Choose the grievance type and enter subject and message body
Submit the ticket by entering "Submit"
