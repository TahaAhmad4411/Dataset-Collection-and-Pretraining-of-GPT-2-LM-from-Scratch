Chairman, Federal Service Tribunal
Mr. Justice (R) Rooh-ul-Amin Khan has assumed the charge as Chairman on 7
th
July, 2023. Before this, he has served as Judge of Peshawar High Court from March 2014 to March 2023. He has retired as Chief Justice of Peshawar High Court. He hails from Chamkani, Peshawar and was born in a prominent family on 01.04.1961. He started his elementary and secondary education from Government High School, Chamkani. He graduated from Government College Peshawar. He availed his LL.B degree from the prestigious Khyber Law College, Peshawar in 1985. He became the member of NWFP Bar Council in the same year.
