Roster Of Vacation Duties Of District / Sessions And Additional District / Session Judges
15 Dec 2022
The Hon'ble Chief Justice Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Court is pleased to approve the
roster for duty as vacation judges for District & Sessions Judges and Additional District & Sessions Judges to dispose of urgent Civil and Criminal matters including regular bail applications filed under section 497 Cr.PC, succession Eruficates certificates and guardianship certificates during the winter vacations are commencing from 16-12-2022 to 15-2-2023 at the District headquarters as specified in the attached notification.
